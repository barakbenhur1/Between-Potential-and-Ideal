AI as Witness

In this project, AI may help reflect, organize, compare, and question. It is not treated as the owner of lived experience or the final source of meaning.

AI is not the living source

The project distinguishes between a tool that can process language and the living source from which experience arrives. AI can produce fluent forms, but it does not suffer, remember, love, fear, risk, or return to a body after the answer is given.

This does not make AI useless. It makes the boundary important. The living person remains prior to the extracted sentence.

Mirror and witness

As a mirror, AI can show a pattern back to the reader. As a witness, it can help hold a question in view long enough for a person to see it again. A witness does not own the truth; it remains answerable to what it observes.

A tool under responsibility

AI becomes helpful when it remains a tool under human responsibility. It can draft, compare, search, structure, and test. It should not replace the moral distance through which understanding becomes one’s own.

The danger of bypass

The danger is not only wrong answers. The deeper danger is bypass: receiving an answer without passing through the distance needed to understand what the answer costs, excludes, or makes possible.

For that reason, this site treats AI as useful, limited, and criticizable. It can assist the path, but it cannot become the path itself.
