## Segment review gate

1. Preserve the opening caution that mathematics and physics do not prove the theory.
2. Keep imaginary numbers as an additional descriptive axis, not metaphysical objects.
3. Keep virtual particles from being portrayed as ordinary hidden particles or as Potential itself.
4. Preserve physical vacuum as distinct from philosophical nothingness without claiming hidden creatures or stored objects.
5. Keep Potential as an active layer or condition of possibility, not another object in the box.
6. Preserve the triad: imaginary as direction, virtual as influence, real as sign.
7. Keep Ideal as appearance that does not betray its source in Potential.
8. Review all imaginary, virtual, vacuum, object, field, condition, Potential, Ideal, and sign terminology before publication.
