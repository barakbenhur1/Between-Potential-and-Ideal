## Segment review gate

1. Preserve the market as an information system and mirror, never as a conscience or god.
2. Keep healthy and sick debt distinct by whether obligation opens or consumes possibility.
3. Preserve interest as a bounded relation among time, inflation expectations, risk, liquidity, and policy conditions.
4. Keep labor embodied, temporal, and testimonial; do not reduce it to output or cost.
5. Distinguish profit from creation and profit from extraction.
6. Preserve externality as cost excluded from the transaction price.
7. Review all market, credit, debt, interest, labor, wage, profit, extraction, and externality terminology before publication.
