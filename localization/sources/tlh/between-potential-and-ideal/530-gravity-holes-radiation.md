## Segment review gate

1. Keep gravity as an exemplary physical structure, never as will, morality, or intention.
2. Preserve limitation as a possible condition of world and freedom without treating all limits as good.
3. Keep black holes as extreme geometric contraction, never evil, punishment, or moral agents.
4. Keep white holes explicitly hypothetical/limited images, never proof of creation or Potential itself.
5. Preserve the popular virtual-particle account of Hawking radiation only as an instructional image, not a literal photograph of the mechanism.
6. Keep vacuum, particle, radiation, observer, and horizon statements scientifically cautious.
7. Preserve structural precedence without claiming unactualized possibilities are “more real” than actual reality.
8. Review all gravity, contraction, horizon, black-hole, white-hole, radiation, particle, observer, and appearance terminology before publication.
