## Segment review gate

1. Preserve redundancy as designed resilience, not waste by default.
2. Keep maintenance as a continuing agreement with time; reject fantasies of maintenance-free redemption.
3. Preserve failure as excluded reality returning, without implying every failure has one cause.
4. Keep accessibility, visibility, light, labor, and circulation as ethical spatial consequences.
5. Preserve beauty as right relation, not decoration or utility alone.
6. End with repair rather than redemption and with human potential held under real conditions.
