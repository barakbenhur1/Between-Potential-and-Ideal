## Segment review gate

1. Preserve legality and worthiness as necessary but non-identical dimensions.
2. Keep harmed person and accused as living sources without creating a false symmetry between their legal roles.
3. Preserve plea bargains as potentially responsible translations or potentially coerced appearances of agreement.
4. Keep punishment distinct from revenge and pain distinct from proof of justice.
5. Preserve the chapter's principled opposition to capital punishment exactly as an argument against final erasure and irreversible state error.
6. Do not weaken society's duty to protect itself or imply that every person can safely return to free social life.
7. Review all legality, worthiness, living-source, plea, punishment, correction, protection, and final-erasure terminology before publication.
