## Segment review gate

1. Preserve value as relational without implying that it is imaginary.
2. Keep money as a medium/sign of recognized potential, not value itself.
3. Preserve the distinction among commodity, representative, and fiat money.
4. Keep purchasing-power and real-exchange-rate formulas directionally and contextually bounded.
5. Treat M × V = P × Y as an identity, not a simple causal theory of inflation.
6. Review all value, money, price, trust, fiat, gold, purchasing-power, exchange-rate, and inflation terminology before publication.
