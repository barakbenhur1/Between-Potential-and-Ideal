## Segment review gate

1. Keep God as a boundary-name for potential, not a factual supernatural claim.
2. Preserve the prohibition against using suffering as proof or justification.
3. Keep multiplicity as encounter without erasing difference or responsibility.
4. Preserve divine risk as consequence and freedom, not glorified pain.
5. Review all theological vocabulary for clarity before publication.
