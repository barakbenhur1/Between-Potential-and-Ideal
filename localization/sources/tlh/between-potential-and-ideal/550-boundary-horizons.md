## Segment review gate

1. Preserve a horizon as a relation among observer, tool, condition, and field of possibilities, not merely an edge or wall.
2. Keep inaccessibility distinct from nonexistence in education, law, medicine, physics, AI, art, and governance.
3. Preserve double humility exactly: do not erase and do not fantasize.
4. Treat the action-under-a-horizon relation as a responsibility map, never an empirical formula.
5. Keep physical event horizons and cosmic event horizons scientifically bounded and distinct from metaphorical uses.
6. Preserve the domain-specific horizons of evidence, understanding, diagnosis, and explanation.
7. End with the duty not to lie about what one does not know, not with a demand for total knowledge.
8. Review all horizon, observer, tool, condition, accessibility, uncertainty, correction, projection, and authority terminology before publication.
