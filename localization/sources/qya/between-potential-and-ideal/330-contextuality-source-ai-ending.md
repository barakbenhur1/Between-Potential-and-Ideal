### 8. Context lá ná subjectivity

Sina ná i heart i capítulo. Context lá ná subjectivism. Lá mean `ilya quén harya truth immo`. Lá mean lá ëa reality pella interpretation. Lá mean ilya interpretation equal. Yando lá mean observer freely creates values willnen.

Quantum contextuality ná technical concept. I broader use i quetë `context` mi chapters AI, law, education, medicine ar art ná cautious structural extension, lá proof transferred physicsello. Capítulo mauya quetë sina tenna lá lanta mir i very mistake ya warns against.

Still, i structural inspiration ná strong: lúmessë lá polë speak responsibly resulten ú speaking i maquetta, instrument, procedure ar frame yassë appeared. Context lá abolish truth; maquetë men ná more precise i form appearanceo.

Truth lá disappear írë appears inside context. Becomes responsible only írë contextrya lá hidden.

### 9. Potential, Ideal ar Optimal inside conditions appearanceo

Potential lá ná warehouse ready-made resultion. Ná field yassë possibility ahya sayable only írë ëa maquetta, instrument, context ar boundary. Sina lá mean ilya nat possible, ar lá mean lá ëa truth until someone looks. Mean possibility takes form under conditions.

Ideal lá ná contextless truth. Ideal ya lá recognizes context immo polë ahya violent; Ideal ya dissolves entirely ana context loses spine. Responsible Ideal holds both: truth ar conditions appearanceo.

Optimal lá ná `relative truth ninyë`. Lá ná subjectivism. Ná i most responsible answer ya polë ná carried inside defined conditions appearanceo: answer ya quetë mana ista, under mana maquetta, by mana instrument, mi mana language, inside mana boundary, ar mana remains open.

Sís capítulo joins previous ones. Mi science parnalmë domain of validity. Mi computation representation ar evidence. Mi structure universeo horizon. Mi high-energy physics scale ar appearance. Sís parnalmë context: lá ve excuse, mal ve condition responsibilityo.

### 10. Living source, distance ar evidence

Living source lá ná quén ya cancels physics favor consciousnesso, ar lá divine observer ya creates truth willnen. Living source ná quén ya maquetë, measures, interprets, errs, corrects ar carries responsibility inside language, body, instrument ar limitation.

Evidence lá ná naked result. Passes through distance: systemello ana measurement, measurementello ana sign, signello ana model, modello ana interpretation, ar interpretationello ana action ya polë ná carried responsibly.

Distance sina lá ná er failure. Ná i ya makes criticism possible. Qui lá ëa distance imbe result ar interpretation, lá ëa tië testing interpretationo. Qui lá ëa distance imbe source ar output, lá ëa tië asking mana né lost mi i road.

I theory an sina lá seeks abolish distance. Seeks avá pretend distance absent. Responsibility yesta írë one identifies i path signello ana meaning.

### 11. AI: output depends context

AI lá ná non-local mi physical sense, ar quantum mechanics lá mauya ná used explainien operationrya. Mal AI tana clear manen output depends context: prompt, training data, system policy, memory, tools, interface, language, culture, user intent, evaluation metric ar deployment environment.

`I model said` an sina lá ná end discussiono mal beginning. Closer ná ana `i detector registered` than `truth yá spoken`. Mauya maquetë: mana model? mana prompt? mana information? mana constraints? mana use? mana source? mana test? mana responsibility?

Output lá appears mi vacuum. Ná result system conditionso. Ve throughout capítulo, context lá abolish possibility evaluating i answer; maquetë evaluation more precise. AI answer polë ná useful, mal only if lá nurtë conditions appearanceo.

Optimal mi working ó AI lá ná answer ya hlarë most confident. Ná answer ya polë show conditions, limits, sources, purpose ar i human being ya carries responsibility useo.

### 12. I recursive edge: man defined i context?

Írë context answero ná exposed, responsibility lá ends. Maquetta vinya panta: man defined context, mi mana context né defined, mana lavë men cenë, ar mana nurtë? Sina ná recursive edge trutho inside conditions appearanceo.

Ilya measurement frame rests on another frame. Ilya language rests on wider language. Ilya tool rests on design choices. Ilya AI policy rests on values. Ilya legal procedure rests on assumptions justiceo. Ilya exposed context opens another context.

Sina lá abolish truth. Prevents truth disguising immo ve view without place. Recursive edge lá ná despair; ná form responsibilityo sanwën ya lá lies immon that finished.

Capítulo an sina lá ends quetien everything depends everything. Ends ó opposite demand: qui nat depends context, mauya become more precise, lá less.

Locality, non-locality ar contextuality lá prove i theory. Parmar form responsibilityo. Locality enyalë ilya action begins somewhere. Non-locality enyalë lá ilya correlation ná message ar lá ilya relation communication. Contextuality enyalë context lá ná noise ara truth, mal lúmessë ranta conditions appearanceo.
