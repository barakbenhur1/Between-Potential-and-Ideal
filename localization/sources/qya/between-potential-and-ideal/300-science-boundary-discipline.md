## Science, Physics ar Mathematics ve Boundary Discipline

*Mana ná claim, mana ná model, mana ná metaphor, ar mana lá mauya ahya proof*

![Science, Physics, and Mathematics as Boundary Discipline](../../../figures/chapter-science-physics-math-boundary-v2.png)

_Image description draft: formulas, measurement ar models ve boundary languages, lá final proof._

### Manen loë precision ú carien sa decoration

Science, physics ar mathematics tulir mir i theory lá antien aura authorityo, mal parmien discipline. Enyalir sa ilya form responsible mauya ista yassë carë, yassë polë ná metaina, yassë lanta, ar mana lá mauya claim beyond i ya né shown. Ter i lens i theoryo, science lá ná i Ideal immo, ar mathematics lá ná substitute truth cuilëo. Nar boundary languages: ways precise hyanien possibility, form, test, validity ar limitation.

Scientific Potential ná i field possibilities ya en lá samë formulation stable: phenomenon en úexplained, relation en úmeasurable, pattern ya repeats mal en lá understood, hya maquetta yassen lá ëa tool adequate. I scientific Ideal lá ná final answer, mal i formulation responsible amba i ya polë ná known under conditions antainë, tested, falsified, repeated ar corrected. I scientific Optimal ná i local model ya carë under conditions defined ú pretending ná absolute truth. Good science an sina lá ná er power knowledgeo; ná yando nostale humilityo.

I failure yesta írë precision ahya decoration. Apa formula, diagram, term physical hya concept mathematical tulë cenna, i reader polë felë i claim philosophical né proven. Tana exactly i ya lá mauya martya sís. Formula mi theory sina polë ná relation map, structural image hya clarifying device, mal lá ná scientific formula unless harya units, measurement, domain validityo, testing conditions ar possibility failureo. Otherwise lá ná proof. Ná lambë.

Neldë levels mauya ná kept distinct. Metaphor loë concept illuminien similar structure. Model organizes relations mi way whose coherence polë ná examined. Scientific claim maura conditions tulcë amba: definition, measurement, source, method, domain validityo ar ability to fail. Confusing levels sin ná central danger philosophical writing ya borrows scientific language. Onta feeling deptho ú colien i haiya ya depth maura.

> **Caution map draft:** definition × domain validityo × distinction metaphor/modelo × source × possibility criticismo ÷ borrowed authority × field-mixing × grand words × pretended proof.

Ilya use QFT, Higgs, Bell, Kochen-Specker, Bohmian mechanics, contextuality, locality hya non-locality mauya lelya ter map sina. Qui term lá carë real work, lá mauya appear. Qui appear, mauya lemya loyal boundaryryan.

Mathematics parma i theory distinctions yar lá polë lose: possibility lá ná proof; proof lá ná computation; computation lá ná understanding; existence lá ná construction; verification lá ná decision; ar local solution lá ná closure i fieldo ilya. P versus NP, Gödel, Turing, recursion ar computational hierarchies lá prove i theory. Restar sa quetien carefully systems, boundaries, verification, closure ar i point yassë language lá polë contain immo completely.

Physics parma humility hyana. Theory polë ná precise very mi domain er ar still partial. Model polë carë well, fail mi edge, ná extended, hya ahya limiting case languageo núra. Sina lá ná weakness scienceo mal er strengthryaron: ista cuita ó domain validityo. Sillo i theory para i real Optimal lá pretend ná absolute Ideal. Quetë: sís carin; sís en lá istan.

Mi sense sina, boundary discipline ná form moral responsibilityo. Írë scientific language ná used ve decoration, exploits i work scientistswa ve effect rhetorical. Mapë result distanceo, experiment, failure, repetition, criticism ar measurement, ar carë sa ornament. Mal írë used carefully, airita i source cuina yallo knowledge tullë: people, institutions, experiments, instruments, errors ar corrections.

Sina especially important mi theory Potentialo ar Idealo. Ná easy quetë `ilya ná potential`, `ilya ná field`, `ilya ná information`, hya `ilya ná relation`. Quettar sin ahyar dangerous írë autar distinctions. Potential lá ná excuse vaguenesso. Ideal lá ná essë hyana ilya nat ya hlarë deep. Optimal lá ná formula ya replaces judgment. Ilya concept mauya colë workerya inside boundary clear.

An sina capítulo lá cesta turn i theory ana science, ar lá cesta prove sa ter physics hya mathematics. Maquetë nat modest amba ar difficult amba: lambë ya lá steal authority elsewhere. Írë i theory loë science, mauya quetë ma quetë ve metaphor, model, structural comparison hya claim. Qui lá polë quetë sina, mauya lemya silent tenna polë.

I connection ana recursive edge ná calima: ilya answer precise panta boundary vinya. Ilya model successful yando tana mana lá contain. Ilya proof tarë inside system. Ilya measurement dependë instrument, context ar question. Science, írë faithful immon, lá ná cotumo estelo ar lá replacement sen. Ná min i tier yassen human potential tulë arin ana Ideal ú quetila immon yá arrived.

### Boundary rules

- science = discipline, lá borrowed authority.
- formula lá ná proof by itself; scientific claim maura units, measurement, domain, test ar possibility failureo.
- metaphor, model ar claim nar levels distinct.
- mathematics parma distinctions, lá prove i theory.
- physics airita domain validityo, partiality ar edge failure.
- ilya concept mauya colë workerya mi boundaryrya.

> **Closing sentence draft:** Precision harya honor írë ista boundaryrya; írë ahya decoration, steals authority ar autë truth.

## Segment review gate

1. Keep science, physics, and mathematics as discipline, not external proof.
2. Preserve the difference between metaphor, model, structural comparison, and claim.
3. Require scientific claims to carry definition, measurement, source, method, domain, and failure conditions.
4. Keep all named scientific terms loyal to their boundaries.
5. Normalize all science, proof, model, metaphor, claim, and domain terminology under one Neo-Quenya profile.
