# Segment 020 stopping-before-control gate — round 2

Status: bounded restraint test. No production wording selected. Production source unchanged. Publication forbidden.

This record tests the source requirement that responsible help stops before it becomes control. Stopping, control, protection, limitation, and non-replacement remain distinct concepts.

# Neo-Quenya (`qya`)

## Stop direction

The pinned Late-Quenya course glossary records unmarked:

- `pusta-` — “to stop”.

Because the entry is unmarked in that course, it is treated there as attested rather than reconstructed or fan-invented. However, the exact Tolkien primary-source locator has not yet been entered in this project record.

## Bounded research test

`Prime Intellect pusta.`

Parse:

- `Prime Intellect` — protected proper-name subject;
- `pusta` — singular aorist test form of `pusta-`, “stops”.

Literal back-translation: **Prime Intellect stops.**

## Decision

The form is retained as a **research test only**, not as a source-closed clause.

It does not yet state:

- what action stops;
- when the stop occurs;
- what would count as control;
- that stopping is voluntary;
- that the living participant benefits;
- that Prime Intellect does not resume the person's action under another description.

The following remain unresolved:

1. exact primary locator and period for `pusta-`;
2. a source-compatible construction for “before” in this clause;
3. a source-compatible, context-specific expression for control or takeover;
4. a full parse proving that Prime Intellect is the actor who stops.

Neo-Quenya gate:

- stop direction identified: **yes**;
- exact primary locator entered: **no**;
- before-control construction complete: **no**;
- production use allowed: **no**.

# Klingon (`tlh`)

## Canonical directions

- `mev` — stop, cease; TKD lexical entry;
- `SeH` — control; TKD lexical entry;
- `-pa'` — subordinate-clause suffix “before”; TKD Type 9 verb suffix.

## Controlled test

`SeHpa' Prime Intellect, mev Prime Intellect.`

Parse:

Subordinate clause:

- `SeH-pa'` — control + before;
- `Prime Intellect` — third-person subject;
- literal: “before Prime Intellect controls”.

Main clause:

- `mev` — stops/ceases;
- `Prime Intellect` — third-person subject.

Literal back-translation:

**Before Prime Intellect controls, Prime Intellect stops.**

Controlled contextual back-translation:

**Prime Intellect stops before its help becomes control.**

## Decision

The literal clause passes the internal grammar-role test:

- Prime Intellect is the explicit subject of both actions;
- stopping occurs before control;
- protection, permission, and control are not represented by the same verb;
- the living person is not made the object of `SeH` in this bounded test.

The contextual back-translation is broader than the literal form. The Klingon clause does not itself contain a noun for “help” or a possessive “its”. Therefore production use remains blocked until an external reviewer confirms that the intended discourse connection is sufficiently clear or requires an explicit help clause.

Klingon gate:

- stop lexeme sourced by canonical class: **yes**;
- control lexeme sourced by canonical class: **yes**;
- before construction sourced by canonical class: **yes**;
- subject roles parsed: **yes**;
- internal bounded test passed: **yes**;
- external idiom review complete: **no**;
- production use allowed: **no**.

## Rejection conditions

Reject any later clause if:

- stopping is caused only by inability rather than chosen restraint;
- the control word is reused as protection or help;
- the living participant becomes the object controlled as though control were benign;
- a before-clause hides which actor stops;
- `pusta-` enters production before its primary source and period are recorded;
- the Klingon contextual gloss adds “help” or “its” without target-language support.
