# Linguistic review — 500 Evidence Horizons, Legal AI, and Working Questions

Both drafts preserve the required safeguards: factual, evidentiary, and legal truth remain distinct but related horizons; legal humility remains compatible with decision rather than an excuse to avoid responsibility; AI remains a visible, challengeable scaffold and never a hidden judge; transparency, auditability, appeal, identified human responsibility, documentation, bias testing, access rights, and tool-versus-decision separation remain mandatory; independent AI decisions about guilt, liberty, dangerousness, or entitlement remain prohibited; every working question and the disclaimer that the chapter is not legal advice or a ready legal doctrine remain present; the person remains a living source beyond their role in the case. Neither draft is approved.

Klingon blockers: extensive undeclared English remains in the title, headings, safeguards list, working questions, and vocabulary of evidence horizon, factual truth, evidentiary truth, legal truth, AI, transparency, auditability, appeal, documentation, bias, access rights, automation, guilt, liberty, dangerousness, entitlement, scaffold, decision, legal doctrine, and living source. Hybrid constructions and English-shaped syntax remain pervasive. A governed Klingon profile is required for evidentiary horizons, challengeability, audit, human responsibility, automated assistance, prohibited decisions, and legal disclaimers.

Neo-Quenya blockers: the title, headings, safeguards list, working questions, and nearly all legal-AI terminology remain in ordinary English. English word order and mixed clauses dominate the treatment of evidence horizons, responsibility, transparency, auditability, appeal, bias, access, automation, guilt, liberty, dangerousness, entitlement, and disclaimer language. Case marking, compounds, verbal morphology, and the distinctions among factual, evidentiary, legal, assistive, advisory, and decisional functions require normalization under one Neo-Quenya profile.

Keep both sources as `draft` with `publication: forbidden`.
