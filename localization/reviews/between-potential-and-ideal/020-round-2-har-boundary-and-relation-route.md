# Segment 020 `har-` boundary and relation route — round 2

This record narrows the two blockers identified for the `Cantië` philosophical frame. It approves no Neo-Quenya sentence, title, body wording, segment, or language edition.

## Evidence boundary for `har-`

The pinned Eldamo introductory course records `har-` as “sit, stay” and treats it as an unmarked lexical item while marking the displayed past form `handë` with a reformulation marker. Its location-verb discussion distinguishes several senses:

- `har-` — sit or stay, including a request to remain for a while;
- `ser-` — rest or tarry for a time;
- `mar-` — dwell, abide, or live in a place;
- `coita-` — be alive.

The same discussion describes the location-verb material as based mainly on the 1948 Quenya Verbal System, with the abandoned earlier `ham-` updated to later `har-` from PE17/162 and UT/305.

### Decision

`har-` is **source-supported for sitting or staying in a place or situation**, but **rejected for the identity-persistence slot** in segment 020.

The available evidence does not establish that `har-` can mean:

- remain the same person;
- preserve identity while a relation changes;
- continue to exist as an unreplaced living source;
- remain oneself in a philosophical contrast with substitution.

Using it for those claims would extend a locational or temporary-stay sense into an unverified metaphysical sense. The frame must not make that extension.

## Other remain/preserve directions

- `lemya-`: remains not admitted; draft and fan-composition usage do not establish the required Late-Quenya sense.
- `ser-`: remains rejected; resting or tarrying is not identity persistence.
- `mar-`: rejected for this frame; dwelling or living in a place is not remaining oneself.
- `harya-` or possession-based wording: rejected by policy because the living source must not be represented as an object held by the system.

No remain/preserve verb is admitted for the philosophical frame.

## Relation route decision

No source-approved abstract interpersonal relation noun has been established. `ovantë` remains unsupported and excluded.

Of the open strategies recorded in the prior assessment, the selected research route is now:

> **Two transparent clauses that avoid both an abstract relation noun and an identity-persistence verb.**

This is a route selection, not a translated candidate. The semantic control must separately state:

1. what the governing pattern changes in the interaction between the two living participants; and
2. that neither living participant is replaced, erased, possessed, or made into the other.

The final target-language construction must name its participants and actions directly. It must not hide agency inside a nominal chain, reduce relation to physical distance, or substitute a spatial-stay verb for preservation of identity.

## New lexical and grammar targets

Before a Neo-Quenya sentence may be drafted, exact sources are required for:

- a Late-compatible verb or construction for two persons meeting, responding, acting, or speaking toward one another;
- a transparent way to identify both participants without an unsupported reciprocal pronoun;
- a Late-compatible verb for replace or substitute, or a fully sourced negative clause that states neither participant becomes the other;
- coordination and negation under the selected Late-Quenya profile.

A preposition meaning “between” may describe participant arrangement only after its source, period, and governed form are recorded. It may not by itself be treated as an abstract relation noun.
