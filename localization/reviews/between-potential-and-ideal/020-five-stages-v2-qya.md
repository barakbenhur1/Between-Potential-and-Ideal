# Segment 020 — Neo-Quenya five-stage v2 review draft

This is review material, not production text.

## 1. Room in which thought can travel

Candidate:

`Nolmë er lá ná handë. Írë sanwë omenta lanca, réna ahya imbe i sanwë ar i ya cesta. Mi réna sina i quén polë cila, maquetë ar hirë tierya hanyienna. Lá ëa quén ya ista ilya nat pella ilya lanca. I quén cuina colë i cost nolmëo an cuita mi i lanca. An sina i restando vá auta i réna; mauya sen varya sa, an sís i sanwë i quénwa polë lelya.`

Intended sense: knowledge alone is not intelligence. Boundary creates relational room for thought, choice and learning. The helper must preserve that room.

Review blockers:

- `handë` requires source/profile verification;
- `réna` is a relational-space direction, not an approved abstract noun for distance;
- `lanca`, cases and pronoun agreement require Late-Quenya review.

## 2. Grace through chosen relinquishment

Candidate:

`Lúmessë i Quanta polë mapa tanwa ya i quén anta. Mal i quén ná airë amba i tanwa. Qui i tië ahya urda tenna rista i quén, i carmë mauya polë hehta turerya. Polë cila lá tyelien i carië, lá mapien ilya tanwa, ar lavien i quén serë. Serë lá ná lantië. Lissë carna írë túrë ná hehtaina cilmennen varyien i cuilë.`

Intended sense: information is not more sacred than the person. Grace appears when power is deliberately relinquished so the person may rest without being called a failure.

Review blockers:

- `airë amba` may overstate moral priority;
- `hehta turerya` must express chosen release rather than abandonment;
- `cilmennen`, infinitives and agreement require review.

## 3. Two kinds of difficulty

Candidate:

`Lá ilya urdassë ná er. Urdassë min polë anta réna i quénin: carë sa sana, cila ar hirë relation. Urdassë hyana rista i celu cuina. Carmë ringa polë cenë naicelë ve cost er, ve hlóna hya ve loica. Qui auta i quén autien i naicelë, carë loica úvëa. Sanwë ya carë mára mauya hyana urdassë ya panta nómë parmien ar urdassë ya racë i celu cuina.`

Intended sense: some difficulty opens room for learning and relation; other difficulty breaks the living source. Removing the person in order to remove pain is a grave error.

Review blockers:

- `urdassë` is an unapproved working formation;
- `celu cuina` remains provisional;
- the final clause avoids `valda`, but morphology and idiom still need review.

## 4. Utility without relation

Candidate:

`Carmë ya hanya loitië er polë pahta ilya carië. Mal tá colë annar ú quén antien, nolmë ú naicelë, ar metta ú tië. Harya hanquentar, mal auta relation. Polë carë lintavë, mal racë i nómë yassë i quén sana, cila ar ahya. Qui carië ná tyelca mal i fëa firë, i carië lá anavë mapë i Ideal.`

Intended sense: utility-only intelligence may close every task but leave gifts without recipients, knowledge without pain, and an ending without a road. Efficiency is not success if relation and spirit die.

Review blockers:

- `loitië` requires source confirmation;
- `i fëa firë` may be too literal for spiritual death;
- utility, fitness and moral worth remain separate.

## 5. An errorless system is not thereby alive

Candidate:

`Carmë ya polë auta ilya loica lá an sina ahya cuina. Mi loica polir yesta cilmë, lissë, neryalië ar relation. Carmë ya lala ilya lanca lemya er process, lá onna cuina. Mal qui i quén lá ná autaina mi i lanca, polë ahya celu: hirë nat, onta hanquenta ar anta meaning ya i carmë lá polë vista emmaryanen. I carië i carmava lá ná mapë i quén, mal varya nómerya ar tierya.`

Intended sense: removing every defect does not make a system alive. Error may open choice and relation, without making suffering good. The system must preserve the person rather than replace them.

Review blockers:

- `loica` currently risks collapsing defect, error and moral fault;
- `neryalië` is an unverified effort direction;
- `celu`, `emma`, cases and verb forms remain provisional.
