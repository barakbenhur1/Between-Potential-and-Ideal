Status: source and construction gate only. No production wording selected. Production source unchanged. Publication forbidden.

This record gates paragraph `020-P01` from `020-body-revision-blueprint-r2.md`. It identifies the exact source claims, the reusable sourced components, the unresolved target-language slots, and the constructions that may not return.

## Canonical source control

The tightened English source distinguishes a living source from a processing tool:

1. a living source is a point of view that passes through distance, not merely a producer of data;
2. a tool may formulate, organize, refine, and answer, but precision does not replace lived experience;
3. worth is not identical with problem-solving power;
4. a quick solution may erase distance the source must cross;
5. the helper gives an answer, while the witness preserves how the question was born and returns responsibility;
6. the tool may illuminate friction but may not claim ownership of it;
7. good witness is active restraint: suggest, ask, return responsibility, and stop before help becomes control.

The Hebrew cross-check confirms the same control:

- AI may be mirror, witness, tool, scaffold, accelerator, translator, preliminary diagnostician, or drainage mechanism;
- it is not the owner of the human road;
- replacing distance produces output without formation;
- helping carry distance may make it a deep tool;
- a tool may open possibility but must not steal the place where a person, community, or living source becomes responsible for what was made.

## Source-side parity decision

The English and Hebrew controls agree on the four propositions required for the opening paragraph:

| Proposition | English | Hebrew | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| tool is not living source | explicit | explicit | passed |
| output or precision does not replace lived path | explicit | explicit | passed |
| capability is not moral worth | explicit | explicit through the worthy-AI discipline map | passed |
| responsibility means preserving human action and stopping before control | explicit | explicit | passed |

Source-side semantic parity for the opening proposition is therefore **passed**. This does not close either target-language lexical or grammar gate.

## Shared target construction

Both target languages must express the opening as explicit clauses rather than a compressed philosophical noun chain.

Required roles:

1. **living participant** — bearer of experience, path, cost, and responsibility;
2. **tool or system** — formatter, organizer, helper, or processing mechanism;
3. **capability** — what the tool can do;
4. **moral conduct** — whether the tool preserves agency and knows when to stop;
5. **answer or output** — useful result that must not be confused with formation;
6. **path or action** — remains grammatically with the living participant.

A candidate fails immediately if it makes the system the owner of the person, the experience, the path, or the responsibility.

Gate: **participant control available; path idiom open**.
