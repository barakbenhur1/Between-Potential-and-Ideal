# Segment 020 external specialist review packet — round 2

Status: external-review request packet. Production source unchanged. Publication forbidden.

This packet asks external Neo-Quenya and Klingon specialists to decide only the bounded questions that remain after internal source, context, parity, and adversarial review. It must not be treated as approval, a production translation, or a request for stylistic rewriting without source evidence.

## Reviewer response requirements

For every item, return exactly one decision:

- **PASS** — acceptable under the declared language profile;
- **PASS WITH CORRECTION** — supply corrected form, complete parse, and exact source;
- **REJECT** — explain the grammatical, lexical, semantic, period, or idiomatic failure;
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE** — identify the missing source or unresolved language fact.

Every accepted or corrected form must include:

1. word-by-word or morpheme-by-morpheme parse;
2. exact source locator for each nontrivial root and construction;
3. literal back-translation;
4. idiomatic back-translation;
5. period/profile compatibility;
6. statement of whether the form is attested, reconstructed, adapted, or newly composed;
7. any alternative reading that could undermine the intended meaning.

Do not approve a form merely because it is understandable or resembles a target-language construction.

# Shared semantic control

Both languages must preserve these roles:

- Prime Intellect may speak, formulate, answer, suggest, and assist;
- the living participant remains the actor who travels, chooses, learns, and bears responsibility;
- capability is not moral worth;
- grace or mercy is restraint, not pity or takeover;
- the intelligence stops before help becomes control;
- the person is not replaced, possessed, converted into output, or treated as raw material;
- physical distance is not a substitute for moral and epistemic room.

The target languages may use different structures, but they must preserve the same actors and ethical boundary.

## QYA-07 — nonreplacement

Current internal role sequence:

`Prime Intellect quetë.`

`I coirëa quén lerta lelya tierya.`

This sequence assigns speech to Prime Intellect and the lived path to the participant, but does not literally say “does not replace” or “does not act instead of”.

Please decide:

1. Is explicit role separation sufficient to preserve the source meaning in context?
2. Is there a sourced Late-compatible construction for “instead of”, “take another's place”, or “replace” that applies safely to persons?
3. Can an explicit negative clause be written without falsely claiming that Prime Intellect never acts?
4. Would a direct replacement verb introduce more semantic risk than the role-based sequence?

Rejected shortcuts:

- non-identity only: `Prime Intellect lá ná i coirëa quén`;
- overly broad inactivity: `Prime Intellect lá carë` or equivalent;
- possession of the person;
- invented compact relation or replacement noun.
