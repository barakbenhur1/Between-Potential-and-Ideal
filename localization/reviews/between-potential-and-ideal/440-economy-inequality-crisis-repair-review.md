# Linguistic review — 440 Inequality, Growth, Crisis, and Economic Repair

Both drafts preserve the required boundaries: inequality becomes structural when unequal outcomes become unequal starting possibilities; bubbles remain tied to self-feeding expectations rather than every price increase; numerical growth remains distinct from growth in human possibility; crisis remains a failure of mediation rather than only loss of assets or output; currency strength remains relational across currencies, goods, labor, time, and purchasing power; the segment ends with economic repair rather than redemption and keeps money and price distinct from full value and truth. Neither draft is approved.

Klingon blockers: extensive undeclared English remains in the title, headings, formulas, lists, and vocabulary of inequality, bubble, growth, crisis, mediation, productivity, liquidity, debt risk, inflation expectations, confidence, political risk, repair, and human potential. Hybrid constructions and English-shaped syntax remain pervasive. One governed Klingon profile is required for structural inequality, speculative feedback, types of growth, mediation failure, relational currency strength, confidence, repair, and the separation of monetary signs from value.

Neo-Quenya blockers: the title, headings, formulas, list questions, and most economic terminology remain in ordinary English. English word order and mixed clauses dominate the treatment of inequality, bubbles, growth, crisis, mediation, confidence, currency strength, and repair. Case marking, compounds, verbal morphology, and the distinctions among outcome, starting possibility, self-feeding expectation, mediation, human progress, and economic repair require normalization under one Neo-Quenya profile.

Keep both sources as `draft` with `publication: forbidden`.
