# Linguistic review — 410 Maintenance, Failure, Responsibility, and Repair

Both drafts preserve the required distinctions: redundancy is designed resilience rather than waste by default; maintenance remains a continuing agreement between form and time rather than a fantasy of maintenance-free redemption; failure is excluded reality returning without being reduced to one universal cause; accessibility, visibility, light, labor, and circulation remain ethical spatial consequences; beauty remains right relation rather than decoration or utility alone; the segment ends with repair rather than redemption and with human potential held under real conditions. Neither draft is approved.

Klingon blockers: extensive undeclared English remains in the title, headings, formulas, and vocabulary of redundancy, maintenance, failure, responsibility, architecture, accessibility, visibility, circulation, beauty, city, resilience, repair, backup systems, service margins, ventilation, public trust, uncertainty, friction, and wear. Hybrid constructions and English-shaped clause order remain pervasive. Terminology for maintenance access, failure paths, ethical consequences, city conflict, resilience, repair, and real operating conditions requires one governed Klingon profile.

Neo-Quenya blockers: the title, headings, formulas, and most technical and civic vocabulary remain in ordinary English. English word order and mixed clauses dominate descriptions of redundancy, maintenance, failure, accessibility, labor, beauty, city systems, public trust, repair, and redemption. Case marking, compounds, verbal morphology, and the distinctions among resilience, maintenance, repair, failure, ethical consequence, and redemptive fantasy require normalization under one Neo-Quenya profile.
