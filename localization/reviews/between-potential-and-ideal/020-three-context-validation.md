## Neo-Quenya (`qya`)

### Transformation / metamorphosis

- Source-backed direction: `ahya-` “to change” — PM/395, 1950s Quenya.
- Current derived title form: `Ahyaliëo`.
- Short-label test: **not passed**. The root is sourced, but the abstract noun and genitive formation used in the title are not yet documented.
- Philosophical test prompt: “The relation changes without erasing the living source.”
- Literary test prompt: “The climb changes the climber without replacing the mountain.”
- Required result: a documented derivation or a transparent verbal construction that preserves transformation without replacement.

### Governing pattern / code

- Source-backed direction: `cantië` “pattern” — NM/287–288, 1959 Quenya.
- Short-label test: **source sense passed; project sense pending**.
- Philosophical test prompt: “The pattern governs repeated moral choices; it is not software instructions.”
- Literary test prompt: “The path has a pattern that guides without owning the climber.”
- Rejection condition: any construction that reads only as visual design, ornament, or computer source code.

### Grace

- Source-backed direction: `lissë` “grace”; genitive `lisseo` — VT44/18.
- Short-label test: **source sense passed; chapter sense pending**.
- Philosophical test prompt: “Grace may require relinquishing information, completion, or control for the sake of the living source.”
- Literary test prompt: “The mountain is honoured, but the climber is more sacred.”
- Blocking issue: `lissë` alone does not encode voluntary release. A separate transparent release construction is required.

### Witness

- Source-backed direction: `astarmo` “bystander; witness” — PE17/71.
- Short-label test: **lexical source passed; ethical-role test pending**.
- Philosophical test prompt: “The witness stands beside the person, remembers distance, and does not own the experience.”
- Literary test prompt: “The witness protects the place where speed would break the climber.”
- Rejection condition: a form that means only spectator, observer, proof, or evidence.

### Distance

- `haia`: sourced adverb “afar; far away”, not an abstract noun.
- `haiya / Haiyava`: **failed**. The current spelling and nominal inflection are unsupported by the recorded Late-Quenya evidence.
- `hairië`: **deferred**. It is a fan-invented Neo-Quenya form and requires an explicit profile exception plus independent review.
- Short-label test: **no accepted candidate**.
- Philosophical test prompt: “Distance permits thought to move without crushing its source.”
- Literary test prompt: “The scaffold permits ascent; it does not steal the climber’s sky.”
- Required result: a sourced descriptive construction using an attested far/remote direction, or a formally approved and documented Neo-Quenya exception.

### Exceptional intelligence and moral worth

- `handassë` and `handelë`: **deferred** because the recorded sources are Middle Quenya, not the default Late-Quenya profile.
- `valda` for moral worth: **failed under the default profile** because Late Quenya records a conflicting sense “excited; wild”.
- Short-label tests: **no accepted candidates**.
- Philosophical test prompt: “Intelligence becomes worthy when it limits itself for the sake of the living source.”
- Literary test prompt: “The most capable guide knows when not to take the climber’s step.”
- Required result: separate sourced constructions for capacity/intellect and moral fitness; neither may collapse into usefulness.

### Voluntary release, protection, and living source

- Research directions only: `apsen-`, `hehta-`, `cauma`, and an explicitly marked metaphor involving `ehtelë`.
- No form is approved by this worksheet.
- The selected release construction must distinguish chosen relinquishment from accidental loss, defeat, abandonment, forgiveness, and deletion.
- The selected protection construction must preserve agency and remain distinct from control.
- A living-source construction must refer to the living person or origin, not merely a literal spring or abstract information source.

## Klingon (`tlh`)

### Source policy

Every accepted root or construction requires a locator in *The Klingon Dictionary*, *Klingon for the Galactic Traveler*, later confirmed Okrand material, or a sourced KLI canon list. The KLI post-dictionary web list intentionally omits the complete TKD/KGT vocabulary, so absence from that page is not evidence against a word.

### Witness

### Moral worth

- Current direction: `lo'laH`.
- Decision: **failed as an unexamined equivalent of moral worth**.
- Reason: practical usefulness or usability must not be collapsed into moral fitness under responsibility.
- Philosophical test prompt: “A useful machine is not therefore a worthy witness.”
- Literary test prompt: “The guide is worthy because it refuses to take the climber’s step.”
- Required result: a sourced descriptive clause separating capacity, usefulness, honour, and moral responsibility.

### Grace of relinquishment, protection, and control

- No compact project term is approved.
- Use transparent verbal clauses until a sourced construction passes all contexts.
- Grace must include chosen release for the sake of the living source.
- Protection must preserve agency.
- Control must describe replacement of the person’s agency and must not reuse the protection construction.
