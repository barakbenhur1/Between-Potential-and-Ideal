# Segment 020 nonreplacement source trace — round 3

Status: reproducible secondary-index search. No production wording selected. Production source unchanged. Publication forbidden.

## Purpose

The round-2 nonreplacement gate recorded that no safe direct Neo-Quenya lexeme for “replace / instead of / act in another's place” had been established. This trace makes that negative result reproducible against the same pinned Eldamo XML snapshot used for the `tamma` and `pusta-` source trace.

It does not replace direct primary-source access or external specialist judgment.

- upstream repository: `pfstrack/eldamo`;
- pinned commit: `4071c9caa95caca905c96af2505d5252045e2aaa`;
- data file: `src/data/eldamo-data.xml`;

The refined gloss search covered explicit forms and nearby lexical families for:

- replace and replacement;
- substitute;

- supplant;
- exchange / swap / change places;
- successor / succeed;
- stand in for.

An earlier broad pass returned only substring false positives (`fireplace` and “stand in memory”), so the final pass separated those from genuine lexical matches.

## Result

No Late Quenya (`q`) word record matched the refined nonreplacement search.

One Middle Quenya (`mq`) record matched:

- `neuro`;
- noun;
- gloss: “follower, successor”;
- indexed source: `Ety/NDEW.013`;
- indexed derivation: `ndeuro`, `Ety/NDEW.009`.

## Candidate assessment

`neuro` is not admitted for the source clause because:

1. it names a follower or successor, not the relation “does not replace / does not act in another person's place”;
2. succession may imply that one actor follows another in office or sequence, which is not the intended ethical boundary;
3. it does not supply an explicit negative nonreplacement construction;
4. its indexed period is Middle Quenya, while the selected profile is Late-Quenya-first;
5. using it would introduce a new person-role noun and additional semantic commitments instead of preserving the original actor division.

Therefore the direct lexical gate remains closed.

## Bounded decision

- reproducible `q` and `mq` gloss search complete: **yes**;
- safe Late Quenya replacement lexeme found: **no**;
- safe “instead of / take another's place” construction found: **no**;
- `neuro` admitted: **no**;
- role-based nonreplacement architecture retained: **yes**;
- explicit negative nonreplacement clause complete: **no**;
- production use allowed: **no**.

The controlled role sequence remains the only internally defensible architecture:

- Prime Intellect performs speech or assistance;
- the living participant performs the lived and morally formative action;
- no clause transfers the participant's path, agency, or responsibility to Prime Intellect.

The external Neo-Quenya specialist must decide whether this explicit role division is sufficient in context or provide a separately sourced construction. A compact replacement verb must not be invented from this negative search result.
