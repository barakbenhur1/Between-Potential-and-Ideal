## Semantic control

- Prime Intellect can produce language, speech, or an answer;

# Klingon (`tlh`)

## Candidate output actions

Two Okrand-canonical lexical directions remain distinct:

- `jatlh` — say, speak;
- `jang` — answer, reply.

Both are lexical entries in *The Klingon Dictionary*. They must not be merged into a single general “produce output” verb.

### Speech test

`jatlh Prime Intellect.`

Parse:

- `jatlh` — verb, “speaks/says”;
- `Prime Intellect` — third-person subject following the verb;
- no object is claimed.

Literal back-translation: **Prime Intellect speaks.**

### Answer test

`jang Prime Intellect.`

Parse:

- `jang` — verb, “answers/replies”;
- `Prime Intellect` — third-person subject following the verb;
- no answer content or recipient is claimed.

Literal back-translation: **Prime Intellect answers.**

## Decision

For the opening proposition's broad dialogue control, `jatlh Prime Intellect` is the preferred test because it states participation in language without implying that a question-and-answer event has already been introduced.

`jang Prime Intellect` remains available only where the source specifically contrasts an answer with formation or witness.
