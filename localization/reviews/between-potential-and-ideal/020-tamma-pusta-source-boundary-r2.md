## Reliability-marker control

The pinned Eldamo introductory course explains its vocabulary markers as follows:

- unmarked items are attested in Tolkien's writing;
- `#` marks a base extracted from an inflected form;
- `*` marks a deduced form;
- `^` marks a form adapted from earlier or rejected material;
- `!` marks a fan-invented form.

An unmarked course entry is therefore stronger than a reconstructed or fan-created form, but the project still requires the exact primary locator, period, sense boundary, and clause-level parse before production use.
