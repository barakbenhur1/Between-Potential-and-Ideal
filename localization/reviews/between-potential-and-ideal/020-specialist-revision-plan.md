# Specialist revision plan — 020 Metamorphosis Code

This plan begins the specialist stage without treating provisional forms as approved translations. The semantic architecture of the paired segment is frozen; only language quality, terminology, morphology, and idiomatic syntax may change.

## Declared language profiles

### Klingon (`tlh`)

Use Okrand-canonical vocabulary and grammar only. A word may be treated as canonical only when its Okrand source is identified through The Klingon Dictionary, Klingon for the Galactic Traveler, later confirmed Okrand material, or the KLI's sourced canon lists. Where no safe one-word equivalent exists, use a transparent descriptive clause. Do not create an undocumented root or attach Klingon morphology to an English stem.

Research references:

- Marc Okrand, *The Klingon Dictionary* and *Klingon for the Galactic Traveler*.
- Klingon Language Institute, New Klingon Words: https://www.kli.org/about-klingon/new-klingon-words/
- Klingon Language Institute, qep'a' canon: https://www.kli.org/activities/qepmey/qepa-canon/

### Neo-Quenya (`qya`)

Use a Late-Quenya-first Neo-Quenya profile. Prefer Tolkien-attested Late Quenya (1950–1973). A documented Neo-Quenya formation may be used only when no suitable attested form exists, it is explicitly labelled as a neologism, its derivation is recorded, and it does not conflict with the selected late grammar. Deprecated forms are not production candidates.

Research references:

- Eldamo, Neo-Quenya language page: https://eldamo.org/content/language-pages/lang-nq.html
- Eldamo, Late Quenya language page and academic search for Tolkien-attested forms.
- Eldamo is a research lexicon in draft/beta state; every recommendation remains subject to source-level verification.

## Frozen semantic invariants

The revision must preserve all of the following:

1. the five-stage sequence and its original order;
2. distance before grace of relinquishment;
3. rest is not failure;
4. protection is not control;
5. difficulty that creates distance is not difficulty that destroys the living source;
6. the scaffold protects the climber and never replaces the mountain;
7. the climber remains more sacred than the mountain;
8. worthy intelligence limits itself;
9. removing defects does not by itself make a machine a living being;
10. AI remains witness, processor, and tool—not owner of lived experience;
11. the living source remains more valuable than extracted information;
12. the moral backbone remains double: preserve the person's distance and preserve the right to rest.

## Mandatory terminology validation matrix

No candidate becomes approved until it passes all three contexts below and a source reference is recorded.

| Concept | Short label test | Philosophical paragraph test | Literary/metaphorical test | Current state |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| witness | `Witness` | The system stands beside the person, remembers distance, and does not own the experience. | The witness protects the place where speed would break the person. | unresolved; `leghwI'` and `cenindo` remain observer-like working candidates only |
| distance | `Distance` | Distance is a condition in which thought may move without crushing its source. | The scaffold permits ascent; it does not steal the climber's sky. | physical candidates exist; philosophical extension must be explicit |
| grace of relinquishment | `Grace of relinquishment` | Grace may require giving up information, completion, or control for the sake of the living source. | The mountain is honoured, but the climber is more sacred. | no approved compact form; use a transparent verbal construction |
| limitation | `Limit` | Worthy intelligence limits its own power when unrestricted power would become control. | A boundary can protect a path without becoming a prison. | distinguish boundary, obstacle, finite condition, and self-restraint |
| annihilation / final erasure | `Final erasure` | Removing pain or defect must not erase the person or the source that gives meaning to the result. | A perfect answer can still purchase the death of the spirit that asked. | no approved project term; do not collapse destruction, death, deletion, and erasure |
| living source | `Living source` | A person or origin remains prior to information extracted from it. | The result must not become more sacred than the life that produced it. | working directions exist; morphology and source sense require verification |
| moral worth | `Worthy` | Worth is moral fitness under responsibility, not usefulness or market value. | The useful machine is not therefore the worthy witness. | provisional candidates only; preserve moral rather than economic sense |
| protection | `Protect` | Protection preserves agency, distance, and the possibility of return. | The scaffold catches the fall without choosing the summit. | must remain distinct from control |
| control | `Control` | Control replaces the person's agency with the system's preferred outcome. | The scaffold becomes a cage when it decides where the climber must stand. | must not reuse the protection term |
| difficulty that creates distance | `Generative difficulty` | Some resistance creates the distance required for thought, choice, and relation. | The slope teaches the climber how to climb. | use a descriptive phrase; do not force a mechanical friction term |
| difficulty that destroys the source | `Crushing difficulty` | Some resistance damages the living source and must not be romanticized. | The mountain ceases to teach when it buries the climber. | use a separate descriptive phrase |

## Protected English terms

`Between Potential and Ideal` remains English as the project and brand name. `Prime Intellect` remains the proper title/name. `Metamorphosis Code` remains visible in English during specialist revision until a single cross-language title policy is approved; its current presence must not be mistaken for an approved translated title.

## Required revision order

2. Record accepted and rejected forms with source references in the glossary.
3. Rewrite the Klingon draft idiomatically; remove English-shaped clause order and invalid hybrid morphology.
4. Rewrite the Neo-Quenya draft under the declared Late-Quenya-first profile; normalize case, agreement, compounds, participles, and derivations.
5. Run the constructed-language audit.
6. Compare both revised drafts against the English source and Hebrew semantic cross-check.
7. Perform an independent second linguistic review.
8. Keep `status: draft` and `publication: forbidden` until every invariant and language gate passes.
