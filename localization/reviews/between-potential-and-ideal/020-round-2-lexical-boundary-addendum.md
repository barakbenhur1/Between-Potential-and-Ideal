# Segment 020 lexical-boundary addendum — round 2

This addendum records exclusions discovered while checking the relational-room and protection frames. It does not approve a target-language phrase.

## Physical room is not relational room

The teaching glossary distinguishes:

- `nómë` — place or location;
- `sambë` — a room in the ordinary physical sense.

Decision:

- retain `nómë` as the safer semantic research anchor for a transparent “place for another” strategy;
- exclude `sambë` from the title and subtitle work unless the source sentence actually refers to a physical room;
- do not use `sambë` to encode moral distance, epistemic space, agency or non-possession;
- any eventual `nómë` construction still requires primary-source tracing, case evidence and natural-syntax review.

Reason:

The chapter requires a condition in which another person may think, act, refuse and remain other. A word for an enclosed physical room would narrow and distort that meaning.

## “Beside” is not yet ethical accompaniment

The teaching glossary records `ara` for “next to”.

Decision:

- retain `ara` only as a spatial relation research direction;
- do not treat it as proof that the witness role has been translated;
- the completed witness sentence must separately express non-possession, preservation of agency and refusal to replace experience;
- the governed construction after `ara` remains to be verified.

## Protection verb boundary

The teaching glossary records `varya-` as “protect”, while an example past-tense form is explicitly marked as reconstructed.

Decision:

- retain only the lexical direction `varya-` for further source tracing;
- do not copy the reconstructed past form into a production sentence;
- derive no tense, participle or agent noun until the relevant morphology is sourced and reviewed;
- continue to keep `varya-` distinct from `cauma`, whose recorded project source and nominal scope are different.

## Powerful-actor exclusion

The teaching glossary marks `turwa` for “powerful” with reconstruction notation.

Decision:

- exclude `turwa` from the current title and sentence-frame candidates;
- do not use an uncertain adjective merely to make the semantic frame look complete;
- identify the actor through a sourced concrete noun phrase, such as the system or intelligence under discussion, or leave the slot unresolved;
- capability and moral fitness must remain separate even after the actor is named.

## Pronoun and noun inflection boundary

The same glossary records:

- `hye` — another person, pronoun;
- `quén` with stem `quen-` — person;
- `nómë` — place or location.

These lexical entries do not by themselves validate a combined phrase.

Decision:

- do not infer a dative pronoun from regular noun rules;
- do not publish `hyen` without direct paradigm evidence;
- do not publish a mechanically derived form from `quen-` without case and modifier verification;
- preserve the semantic placeholder `nómë [for another person]` until the full construction is sourced.
