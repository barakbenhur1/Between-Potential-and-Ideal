# Linguistic review — 560 Mass-Energy, Matter, and Medium

Both drafts preserve the required scientific and philosophical boundaries: mass-energy does not prove the theory and physics does not become moral doctrine; matter is not loosely described as metaphysically condensed energy; physical relations remain conditional and precise; matter and body remain media capable of absorbing, redirecting, filtering, storing, releasing, and articulating without reducing persons to containers; possibility remains distinct from goodness and Ideal from realization of every possibility; Hilbert space remains a limited mathematical image for possible states of a defined system rather than the space of all existence; quantum theory and relativity remain different physical languages; mass, energy, field, geometry, horizon, and information remain relational and bounded; coherence remains disciplined relation among distinct parts; teleological language remains explicitly metaphorical; the ending preserves the Ideal as light that passed through matter and learned not to disperse. Neither draft is approved.

Klingon blockers: extensive undeclared English remains in the title, headings, and vocabulary of mass, energy, matter, medium, field, geometry, coherence, horizon, information, Hilbert space, quantum state, observables, measurement, Hamiltonian, relativity, spacetime, teleology, Whole, Potential, and Ideal. Hybrid constructions and English-shaped syntax remain pervasive. One governed Klingon profile is required for physical relations, mathematical-state language, medium functions, coherence, perspective, teleological metaphor, and source fidelity.

Neo-Quenya blockers: the title, headings, and nearly all physical, mathematical, and philosophical terminology remain in ordinary English. English word order and mixed clauses dominate the treatment of mass-energy, matter, medium, fields, Hilbert space, measurement, relativity, spacetime, coherence, horizons, Whole, Potential, and Ideal. Case marking, compounds, verbal morphology, and the distinctions among physical relation, metaphor, formal state space, medium, result, coherence, and teleology require normalization under one Neo-Quenya profile.
