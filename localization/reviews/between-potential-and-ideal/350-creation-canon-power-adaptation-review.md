# Linguistic review — 350 Canon, Power, and Adaptation

Both drafts preserve the segment's principal boundaries: canon is a living invariant rather than a frozen event list; power and knowledge remain potentials requiring moral judgment; the Captain America/Reed Richards and Watchmen contrasts are explanatory rather than rankings; adaptation as reduction is explicitly a structural image rather than a formal computational claim; The Dark Tower, Randall Flagg, and the rose remain examples of a body of work seeking its center. Neither draft is approved.

Klingon blockers: extensive undeclared English remains, including creative franchise, canon, living invariant, official sequence, studio decision, fan wiki, timeline, universe, version, moral center, intellectual solution, optimization, adaptation, cultural reduction, computational reduction, symbol, and related media vocabulary. One complete English sentence remains in the canon definition, and hybrid forms such as `replace-lu'` and `transfer-lu'` remain unstable. Named works and character names may remain as proper nouns, but the surrounding conceptual vocabulary, compounds, clause structure, and register require full idiomatic recasting under one governed Klingon profile.

Neo-Quenya blockers: the draft retains extensive ordinary English and English-shaped clauses, including Creative franchise, living invariant, studio decision, fan wiki, `what remains`, `same question`, moral center, `no perfect solution`, cultural reduction, computational reduction, `on the surface`, and many mixed constructions. Case marking, possessives, compounds, verbal morphology, comparison structures, and imported media terminology are inconsistent. Terms for canon, invariant, identity, adaptation, fidelity, reduction, symbol, power, knowledge, judgment, and moral center require normalization under one Neo-Quenya profile.
