# Segment 020 title-pattern test set

This document turns the title workbench into an executable specialist-review checklist. It defines the source-side meaning that every Klingon and Neo-Quenya title candidate must preserve. It does not propose, select, or approve a production form.

## Canonical source control

- English title: `The Metamorphosis Code of Grace and Distance`
- Hebrew semantic cross-check: `קוד המטמורפוזה של החסד והמרחק`
- Required conceptual units: transformation, governing pattern, grace with voluntary release, and distance as protected room for thought and agency.
- Required negative distinction: the title must not imply that the living source is replaced, erased, possessed, or controlled.

## Mandatory rejection triggers

Reject a candidate immediately if any of the following is true:

1. `code` reads only as software instructions, visual design, ornament, statute, or coercive command;
2. `metamorphosis` reads as cosmetic alteration, replacement, deletion, or destruction of the living source;
3. `grace` is reduced to sweetness, beauty, pity, mercy alone, or passive forgiveness without chosen release;
4. `distance` is reduced to physical length without room for independent thought, action, and refusal;
5. protection and control use the same construction or become semantically indistinguishable;
6. capacity, usefulness, intelligence, honour, and moral worth collapse into one term;
7. the target-language title requires an explanation longer than the title to recover one of the four required conceptual units;
8. the title passes mechanically but uses an unsupported form, undocumented derivation, incompatible language period, or unverified syntax.

## Pattern A — governing pattern of transformation, grace, and distance

### Short-label control

> Governing Pattern of Transformation, Grace, and Distance

The candidate must keep all four units visible and must make their grammatical relation recoverable without relying on the chapter body.

### Philosophical-context control

> The governing pattern changes the relation without replacing its living source: grace releases control, and distance preserves room for another mind to think and act.

### Literary-context control

> The scaffold serves the climb without becoming the mountain, and it gives the climber height without taking the climber's step.

### Pattern-specific failure conditions

- a compressed noun chain leaves it unclear what changes;
- grace and distance appear to be objects that the code destroys or alters;
- the governing-pattern term reads only as ordinary law or software code;
- the title becomes formally faithful but unnaturally heavy in the target language.

## Pattern B — principle by which grace and distance transform relation

### Short-label control

> Principle by Which Grace and Distance Transform Relation

The candidate may use a verbal or clausal title, but it must retain a governing-principle sense and must not imply that grace and distance are interchangeable.

### Philosophical-context control

> Grace transforms relation by relinquishing possession, while distance protects the source's ability to remain other.

### Literary-context control

> The guide changes the journey by knowing when to step back, so the climber can still meet the mountain directly.

### Pattern-specific failure conditions

- the candidate loses the governing-pattern or repeatability sense;
- voluntary release becomes accidental loss, defeat, abandonment, or deletion;
- distance becomes absence, indifference, exile, or mere remoteness;
- the title sounds like a complete explanatory sentence rather than a usable chapter title.

## Pattern C — rule of changed relation through grace and distance

### Short-label control

> Rule of Changed Relation Through Grace and Distance

The candidate must use `rule` only in the sense of a repeatable moral structure. It must not suggest external enforcement or possession of the person.

### Philosophical-context control

> A worthy system follows a rule of changed relation: it may assist, remember, and protect, but it must not replace the person's agency.

### Literary-context control

> The bridge carries weight and preserves the crossing, yet it does not choose the traveller's destination.

### Pattern-specific failure conditions

- the rule term implies command, punishment, enforcement, or obedience;
- relation is omitted and the title becomes a rule for changing grace or physical distance;
- the target form cannot distinguish protection from control;
- the compactness is achieved by hiding one or more source concepts.

## Current applied-title result

### Klingon

Applied form: `pung chuq je choHmeH chut`

Result against this test set: **not passed**.

Recorded blockers:

- exact lexical locators are incomplete;
- the purpose-clause scope can read as a law for changing grace/mercy and physical distance;
- the title does not yet demonstrate relational distance, voluntary release, or a governing-pattern sense distinct from ordinary law;
- no complete philosophical or literary-context candidate has passed idiomatic review.

### Neo-Quenya

Applied form: `I Cantië Ahyaliëo Lissëo ar Haiyava`

Result against this test set: **failed and revision required**.

Recorded blockers:

- `Haiyava` is unsupported by the recorded source evidence;
- the exact derivation and inflection of `Ahyaliëo` remain undocumented;
- `lissë` does not by itself express voluntary relinquishment;
- the coordinated genitive structure has not passed morphology, syntax, or context review.

## Advancement rule

A pattern may advance only when one fully inflected candidate in each language passes all three contexts, all source and grammar locators are recorded, English and Hebrew semantic parity is confirmed sentence by sentence, and an independent second reviewer accepts the result.

Until then:

- no production title is selected;
- no source title is replaced;
- segment 020 remains linguistically unapproved;
- publication remains forbidden.
