# Linguistic review — 520 Imaginary, Virtual, Vacuum, and Potential

Both drafts preserve the required conceptual boundaries: mathematics and physics are not used as proof of the theory; imaginary numbers remain an additional descriptive axis rather than metaphysical objects; virtual particles are not portrayed as ordinary hidden particles or as Potential itself; physical vacuum remains distinct from philosophical nothingness without hidden creatures or stored objects; Potential remains an active layer or condition of possibility rather than another object in the box; imaginary, virtual, and real remain direction, influence, and sign; Ideal remains appearance that does not betray its source in Potential. Neither draft is approved.

Klingon blockers: extensive undeclared English remains in the title, headings, cautions, and vocabulary of imaginary, virtual, real, number, axis, rotation, phase, oscillation, particle, object, field, condition, physical vacuum, philosophical nothingness, influence, sign, Potential, and Ideal. Hybrid constructions and English-shaped syntax remain pervasive. A governed Klingon profile is required for mathematical description, virtual influence, objecthood, vacuum, condition of possibility, trace, source continuity, and the direction-influence-sign triad.

Neo-Quenya blockers: the title, headings, cautions, and nearly all mathematical and physical terminology remain in ordinary English. English word order and mixed clauses dominate the treatment of imaginary numbers, virtual particles, fields, objects, vacuum, nothingness, possibility, signs, Potential, and Ideal. Case marking, compounds, verbal morphology, and the distinctions among descriptive axis, influence, stable object, condition, trace, and metaphysical claim require normalization under one Neo-Quenya profile.

Keep both sources as `draft` with `publication: forbidden`.
