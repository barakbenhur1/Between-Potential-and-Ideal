## Title result

The current Neo-Quenya title fails round 1 because:

1. `Ahyaliëo` remains morphologically undocumented;
2. `Haiyava` is unsupported for abstract distance;
3. `lissë` does not include chosen relinquishment;
4. the long nominal chain has not passed natural-syntax review.

No replacement string is selected yet. Round 2 must test title structures that avoid an unsupported abstract distance noun and avoid pretending that grace and relinquishment are one lexical item.

The current Klingon title remains a mechanical placeholder. Round 1 cannot validate it without exact Okrand locators and idiomatic tests.
