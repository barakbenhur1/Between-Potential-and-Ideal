## Title consequences

1. `cantië` remains the strongest sourced direction for governing pattern, subject to project-sense tests.
2. `lissë` remains a sourced grace candidate, but chosen release must be expressed separately.
3. `Ahyaliëo` remains unapproved until its derivation and inflection are documented; a transparent verbal title strategy remains preferable to an unsupported nominal chain.
4. `Haiyava` remains rejected and must not appear in a production title.
5. `handassë`, `handelë`, and `valda` must not be promoted into the production text under the default profile.
6. No production Neo-Quenya title is selected in this round.

## Klingon evidence result

The KLI post-dictionary and qep'a' canon pages are useful for later confirmed Okrand material, but they do not reproduce the complete vocabulary of *The Klingon Dictionary* or *Klingon for the Galactic Traveler*. An online-page miss therefore cannot establish that a form is non-canonical.

This round does not close the Klingon locator requirement. Exact dictionary, book, qep'a', or other Okrand-source locators are still required for the title components and the core body terms. No production Klingon title is selected.

## Three-context implications

The next candidate work must test, without editing the production drafts yet:

- `cantië` in a short label, philosophical paragraph, and path/climber metaphor;
- `lissë` together with a separately sourced chosen-release clause;
- `astarmo` in the ethical witness role rather than spectator-only use;
- `apsen-` and `hehta-` against forgiveness, abandonment, accidental loss, and chosen relinquishment;
- `cauma` against agency-preserving protection and control;
- a sourced descriptive distance construction that does not depend on `Haiyava` or the fan-created `hairië`;
- separate expressions for exceptional capacity and moral fitness.
