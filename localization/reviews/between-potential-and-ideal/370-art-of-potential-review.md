# Linguistic review — 370 Art of Potential

Both drafts preserve the required boundaries: source, risk, form, and responsible deletion remain one living relation; imperfections are not treated as automatic proof of depth; AI remains a real creative tool that can either extend or replace formation; canon remains memory tested through time rather than authority by itself; the market remains both enabling and deforming, never the final judge. Neither draft is approved.

Klingon blockers: extensive undeclared English remains in the title, headings, formula, media terms, AI terminology, and the core vocabulary of source, form, effect, formation, deletion, canon, market, and automation. Hybrid forms, direct English compounds, and English-shaped clause order remain pervasive. The draft requires one governed Klingon terminology profile and full idiomatic recasting.

Neo-Quenya blockers: the title, subtitle, formula, and many core terms remain in ordinary English. English word order and calqued clauses dominate much of the draft. Case marking, compounds, verbal morphology, and the vocabulary of art, source, formation, deletion, market, canon, and automation require normalization under one Neo-Quenya profile.

Keep both sources as `draft` with `publication: forbidden`.
