# Segment 020 agency/path source assessment — round 2

This assessment narrows the agency-preservation slot in the source-grounded dialogue frame. It approves no Neo-Quenya sentence, title, body wording, segment, or language edition.

## Canonical English control

The English source repeatedly distinguishes witness and protection from taking over the human path:

- worthy intelligence preserves the human being as source rather than product;
- it stands beside the human being;
- the scaffold enables the climb but does not steal movement;
- intelligence must not perform the human being's task or erase the distance only that person can travel.

The agency clause therefore does not need an abstract noun equivalent to “agency”. A direct path clause may preserve the source meaning more transparently.

## Candidate lexical architecture

The pinned Eldamo introductory course records the following unmarked vocabulary directions:

- `tië` — path, road;
- `lelya-` — travel;
- third-person animate possessive `-(r)ya` — his or her, using `-rya` after a vowel;
- animate reference applies to living beings rather than only human males or females.

The course marks unmarked vocabulary as attested somewhere in Tolkien's writings. That attestation status is enough to retain these items for source tracing, but not enough for this project's exact-locator gate.

## Selected research frame

> **The living source travels its own path.**

This English control is selected for source testing because it:

- names the living participant as grammatical subject;
- keeps movement with that participant rather than with the intelligence;
- avoids a speculative verb for “choose”;
- avoids possession of the person by the intelligence;
- matches the chapter's path, mountain, scaffold and distance metaphors;
- can be tested independently from the two directed-speech clauses.

It is not yet a target-language sentence. In particular, the project must not automatically combine the recorded stems into a polished-looking form before each form and inflection has an exact source decision.

## Open source and grammar gates

Before a Neo-Quenya clause may be drafted, record:

1. the exact original Tolkien locator and period for `tië` in the path/road sense;
2. the exact original Tolkien locator and period for `lelya-` in the travel sense;
3. the exact source basis and selected profile for the third-person animate possessive attached to a vowel-final noun;
4. the approved target-language participant phrase for “living source”;
5. the intended tense and whether a generic aorist is semantically preferable;
6. a literal parse proving that the source, not Prime Intellect, is the traveller and possessor of the path.

## Rejected shortcuts

- `cil-` or another “choose” verb is not required and remains outside the frame until independently sourced.
- `immo` is not required merely to force an English-style “own”; a normal possessive construction should be tested first.
- `cuina ehtelë` remains unapproved because `ehtelë` is literally a water spring or issue.
- `har-`, `lemya-`, `ser-`, `mar-`, `harya-` and `ovantë` remain excluded from this frame.
