# Segment 020 agency/path source assessment — round 2

This assessment narrows the agency-preservation slot in the source-grounded dialogue frame. It approves no Neo-Quenya sentence, title, body wording, segment, or language edition.

## Canonical English control

The English source repeatedly distinguishes witness and protection from taking over the human path:

- worthy intelligence preserves the human being as source rather than product;
- it stands beside the human being;
- the scaffold enables the climb but does not steal movement;
- intelligence must not perform the human being's task or erase the distance only that person can travel.

The agency clause therefore does not need an abstract noun equivalent to “agency”. A direct path clause may preserve the source meaning more transparently.

## Primary lexical and grammar evidence

### `tië` — path, road, way

The pinned Eldamo Late-Quenya entry records `tië` with primary references including LotR/377; PE17/13, 72, 76; PE19/71; PE23/134; RGEO/58–59; UT/22; VT39/20; and VT47/11.

The source record distinguishes related senses including path, road, way, line, route and direction. The present frame retains only the concrete-to-literary “path/way” direction. The attested form `tielyanna` “upon your path” at UT/22 also confirms that the noun can carry possessive and case suffixes.

Decision: **primary lexical source gate passed** for `tië` as path or way.

### `lelya-` — go, proceed, travel

The pinned Eldamo Late-Quenya entry records `lelya-` as “to go, proceed (in any direction), travel” from Tolkien's 1959–60 material, with the primary locator WJ/363 and the strong past form `lende`.

Decision: **primary lexical source gate passed** for a generic travel or proceed predicate. No past-tense clause is selected here; the frame still requires a tense decision.

### `-rya` — third-person singular possessive

The pinned Eldamo Late-Quenya entry records `-rya` as “his, her, its” with primary references including PE17/57, 67, 76, 130, 132, 190; PE19/102; and VT49/16–17, 38, 48. It is explicitly identified as a third-person singular possessive form.

Decision: **primary possessive source gate passed**. Its attachment to the selected vowel-final noun must still be shown in the final parse rather than assumed from English word order.

## Selected research frame

> **The living participant travels its own path.**

This English control is selected for source testing because it:

- names the living participant as grammatical subject;
- keeps movement with that participant rather than with the intelligence;
- avoids a speculative verb for “choose”;
- avoids possession of the person by the intelligence;
- matches the chapter's path, mountain, scaffold and distance metaphors;
- can be tested independently from the two directed-speech clauses.

It is not yet a target-language sentence. The sourced elements must not be combined until the participant phrase and complete morphology are approved.

## Remaining source and grammar gates

Before a Neo-Quenya clause may be admitted, record:

1. the approved target-language participant phrase for the living human source;
2. the exact noun form to which `-rya` attaches and the resulting segmented form;
3. the intended tense and whether a generic aorist is semantically preferable;
4. subject–verb agreement;
5. a literal parse proving that the living participant, not Prime Intellect, is the traveller and possessor of the path;
6. an idiomatic back-translation and independent review.

## Rejected shortcuts

- `cil-` or another “choose” verb is not required and remains outside the frame until independently sourced.
- `immo` is not required merely to force an English-style “own”; the sourced possessive construction is the preferred test route.
- `cuina ehtelë` remains unapproved because `ehtelë` is literally a water spring or issue.
- `har-`, `lemya-`, `ser-`, `mar-`, `harya-` and `ovantë` remain excluded from this frame.
