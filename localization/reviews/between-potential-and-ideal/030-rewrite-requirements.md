# Segment 030 rewrite requirements

## Klingon

1. Recast English-shaped noun chains into explicit OVS clauses.
2. Record exact canonical sources for signature, echo, processing, imitation, interpretation, contact, experience, trace and mirror.
3. Frame every nonphysical use of `chuq`; never let physical distance silently carry moral or epistemic meaning.
4. Do not use `leghwI'` as the whole ethical-witness concept. Add a clause stating non-ownership, memory of origin and protection of the source.
5. Separate processor, mind, tool and possible living intelligence.
6. Separate calculation from knowledge born through bodily experience.
7. Preserve all six bodily states: hungry, growing, wounded, light-seeking, afraid and persistent.
8. Preserve cat, flower and human exactly.
9. Preserve the black mountain and climber comparison exactly.
10. Keep the consciousness question grammatically open.
11. Keep thin boundary distinct from erased boundary.
12. Preserve rest as a morally permitted response at the breaking point, not failure.

## Neo-Quenya

1. Remove every nominal use of `haiya/haiyava`.
2. Remove `handassë` under the default Late-Quenya-first profile.
3. Do not use `valda` for moral worth.
4. Retain `astarmo` only with an explicit ethical-witness clause.
5. Verify or replace `rinca`, `Cendë`, `carma`, `carindo`, `hanyalië`, `ovantë`, `cuilë-isto` and every derived form.
6. Separate processing, echo, imitation, interpretation, contact and experience.
7. Use contextual clauses for lived distance rather than one unsupported abstract noun.
8. Preserve cat, flower and human exactly.
9. Preserve all bodily states and the price-of-knowing relation.
10. Preserve the black mountain and climber comparison exactly.
11. Normalize case, agreement, compounds, participles, infinitives and word order.
12. Keep AI consciousness explicitly unresolved.
