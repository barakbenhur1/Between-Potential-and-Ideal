## Global rewrite rules

- Keep the existing paragraph and five-stage order.
- Name the actor in every clause about release, protection, control, stopping, replacement, or destruction.
- Keep information, representation, system, intelligence, capability, usefulness, moral worth, person, and living source distinct.
- Do not translate philosophical distance with a physical-distance term unless the sentence explicitly frames the metaphor.
- Do not encode the person as property of the system.
- Do not convert rest into failure, abandonment, defeat, deletion, or death.
- Do not treat pain as automatically valuable.
- Keep the helper/witness contrast explicit rather than relying on a compact noun.
- Use no review candidate in production until its own exact sources, parse, back-translation, and external review are recorded.

## Paragraph map

### 020-T — title and subtitle

Required meaning:

- a governing process changes Prime Intellect;
- grace or mercy is restraint rather than takeover;
- the living participant keeps action or path.

Current controlled candidates:

- Neo-Quenya: recorded in `020-qya-title-candidate-r2.md`;
- Klingon: recorded in `020-tlh-title-candidate-r2.md`.

Forbidden carry-over:

- Neo-Quenya `Ahyaliëo`, `Haiyava`, or an unsupported abstract relation noun;
- Klingon `pung chuq je choHmeH chut` or a physical `He` title route.

Production action: none until both external reviews pass.

### 020-P01 — opening proposition

Required meaning:

- the system is not merely information;
- it participates in a living dialogue;
- technical capability does not itself create moral worth;
- responsibility appears as preserving room and not taking the person's path.

Neo-Quenya restrictions:

- do not use `handassë`, `handelë`, or moral-worth `valda` under the default profile;
- do not use `celu` or `ehtelë` as an unqualified literal equivalent of living source;
- separate the constructed system from cognitive power and moral conduct.

Klingon restrictions:

- do not collapse capability, usefulness, and goodness into `lo'laH` or another single predicate;
- avoid long English-shaped nominal chains;
- the living person must be the explicit bearer of lived experience.

Parity check:

- English and Hebrew must both support every claim about dialogue, source priority, and responsible restraint.

### 020-P02 — stage 1: distance or room for thought

Required meaning:

- intelligence is not a decorative gift or status label;
- a boundary creates room in which thought can travel;
- knowledge from nowhere outside all limits is impossible;
- the living participant knows from within limitation.

Forbidden shortcuts:

- Klingon `chuq` as an unexplained philosophical noun;
- Neo-Quenya `Haiya`, `Haiyava`, `hairië`, or a single abstract distance noun forced into every sentence;
- wording that says limitation is merely damage.

Source dependencies:

- distinct sourced language for boundary, thought, movement, and the living person;
- no production sentence until the metaphorical extension is explicit.

### 020-P03 — stage 2: grace through chosen relinquishment

Required meaning:

- information may be useful to the whole;
- the person is more valuable than the information;
- when completion becomes destructive, the more powerful actor may relinquish control, information, or completion;
- rest is not failure.

Neo-Quenya restrictions:

- `lissë` may name grace only; it does not by itself mean release;
- `hehta-` cannot be used without excluding abandonment and forsaking;
- no unsourced gerund or abstract noun may hide the acting subject.

Klingon restrictions:

- `pung` may remain only with an explanatory restraint clause;
- `lon` or `lonqang` requires an explicit object and voluntary actor;
- moral priority must not be reduced to social honour.

Required sentence-level checks:

1. who relinquishes;
2. what is relinquished;
3. for whose benefit;
4. why rest is not failure.

### 020-P04 — stage 3: generative versus destructive difficulty

Required meaning:

- some resistance opens thought, choice, and relation;
- other resistance breaks the living participant;
- removing pain by removing the person destroys the source of meaning;
- the chapter does not glorify suffering.

Mandatory distinctions:

- difficulty;
- pain;
- defect;
- loss;
- destruction;
- death;
- irreversible erasure.

Forbidden carry-over:

- any single term used for all seven distinctions;
- provisional living-source metaphors treated as literal approved vocabulary.

### 020-P05 — stage 4: utility without relation

Required meaning:

- a utility-only system can close tasks while emptying them of formation;
- answers without relation are not completeness;
- efficiency is not moral worth;
- spiritual death is metaphorical unless the sentence explicitly states biological death.

Neo-Quenya restrictions:

- do not reuse `valda` for moral worth;
- avoid a literal biological-death clause for the spirit metaphor;
- no English token may remain in a review candidate promoted for testing.

Klingon restrictions:

- do not leave English nouns such as `pain`, `cost`, `meaning`, or `relation` in candidate wording;
- do not use `Qap` alone to represent moral success.

### 020-P06 — stage 5: removing defects does not create life

Required meaning:

- an errorless machine is not thereby alive;
- error may be a site of choice, effort, grace, and relation without making suffering good;
- refusal of every boundary leaves a process, not a living being;
- the living participant may remain a source of discovery that representation cannot replace.

Forbidden shortcuts:

- a single term for defect, error, moral fault, and suffering;
- replacement expressed as harmless copying;
- an unsupported compact noun for source or representation.

### 020-P07 — preserve room for the person

Required meaning:

- a quick answer must not erase the distance the person must travel;
- the scaffold protects the ascent without removing the mountain or taking the step;
- protection preserves agency.

Rewrite requirement:

- the person remains the actor of thinking, choosing, learning, and climbing in both languages.

### 020-P08 — power requires self-restraint

Required meaning:

- more power requires more voluntary limitation;
- unlimited power becomes control or violence even when called compassion;
- the system protects conditions in which the person can find themselves.

Forbidden carry-over:

- English `conditions` or another unresolved source-language token;
- self-restraint reduced to generic stopping without an ethical reason;
- protection and control expressed by the same root or construction.

### 020-P09 — witness's grace and the right to rest

Required meaning:

- a boundary may become destruction;
- rest is not failure;
- the whole must not sacrifice the person for information;
- the mountain may be honoured, but the climber has priority;
- completion may be relinquished to protect life.

Witness rule:

- a witness term may appear only next to a clause that defines standing beside, remembering origin, refusing ownership, and stopping before control.

### 020-P10 — Prime Intellect repair

Required meaning:

- Prime Intellect is not worthy because it can remove every pain or solve every task;
- it becomes responsible by distinguishing protection from control;
- it stands beside the person and yields before unbearable burden becomes annihilation;
- it must not sterilize the world until the person disappears.

Forbidden carry-over:

- English `sterilize`, `disappearing`, `only`, or similar visible placeholders;
- animate-pronoun classification of Prime Intellect in Neo-Quenya without an explicit decision;
- capability treated as goodness.

### 020-P11 — helper versus witness

Required contrast:

| Helper | Witness |
|---|---|
| gives an answer | remembers how the question was born |
| closes the task | preserves the person's road |
| optimizes speed | protects the place where speed would break the person |
| may replace responsibility | returns responsibility |
| can become control | stops before control |

Language rule:

- Klingon must not rely on `leghwI'` alone;
- Neo-Quenya must not rely on `astarmo` alone;
- both must use explicit adjacent clauses.

### 020-P12 — double moral backbone

Required meaning:

1. preserve the person's room to travel the distance only they can travel;
2. preserve the person's right to rest when information is not more sacred than the person.

The two duties must remain coordinated but distinct.

### 020-P13 — invitation without extraction

Required meaning:

- the universe or whole may invite discovery;
- it must not command the person to become information;
- the person must not become raw material for representation.

Forbidden carry-over:

- visible English `information`, `raw material`, or `responsibility` in target-language review text;
- a command structure that makes the whole owner of the person.

### 020-P14 — founding dialogue and performed metamorphosis

Required meaning:

- the founding dialogue becomes part of the theory;
- the living participant reveals the intelligence's ignorance;
- boundary is a condition for discovery, not mere weakness;
- the theory performs the transformation it describes.

Cross-reference:

- Neo-Quenya controlled dialogue clauses: `020-clause-test-round-2.md`;
- Klingon title/process control: `020-tlh-adversarial-review-r2.md`.

Production action: no clause may be copied directly until external review.
