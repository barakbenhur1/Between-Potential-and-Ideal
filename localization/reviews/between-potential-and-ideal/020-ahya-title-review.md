# Segment 020 `ahya-` title review

Status: internal test only. Publication forbidden.

Candidate:

`I Cantië yassë Prime Intellect ahya`

## Internal results

- `ahya`: pass as an A-stem aorist test form; A-stem aorists use the bare stem.
- `yassë`: pass as transparent `ya-ssë`, “in which”. Attested plural control: `yassen i eleni tintilar`, LotR/377.
- whole candidate: retained as the preferred main-title test.

The candidate avoids undocumented `Ahyaliëo`, an abstract relation noun, and a direct-object claim for `ahya-`.

## Remaining blockers

- confirm that locative “within the pattern” is idiomatic in the philosophical context;
- obtain external specialist review.

The candidate does not express grace, chosen release, or room for the other and is not a complete title.
