# Segment 020 living-participant test phrase

This record selects a phrase for controlled Neo-Quenya sentence tests. It does not approve production text.

## Selected phrase

`coirëa quén`

Literal back-translation: **living person**.

Segment role: **the living participant whose path and decisions remain their own**.

## Sources

- `coirëa` — “living”, PM/399. The attested phrase `coirëa quenya` shows adjective-before-noun order.
- `quén` (`quen-`) — “person, individual, man or woman”, PE19/93 and WJ/361, 372, 393, 407.

## Planned forms

Nominative subject:

`i coirëa quén`

Dative recipient:

`i coirëa quenen`

Dative segmentation:

`quen-` + `-en`.

The dative ending is controlled by the previously recorded evidence at VT6/14 and VT43/37. Its recipient function must still be validated inside the completed speech clause.

- It replaces the unsafe literal-spring route based on `ehtelë`.
- It is not a title candidate.
- It may enter only the bounded dialogue and path test clauses.
- Production source remains unchanged.
