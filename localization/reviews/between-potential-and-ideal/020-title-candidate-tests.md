## Neo-Quenya

Applied candidate: `I Cantië Ahyaliëo Lissëo ar Haiyava`

Intended working meaning: the pattern of the transformation of grace and distance.

Current test state:

1. **Short heading:** mechanically passes with zero unresolved English findings, but fails source validation for the current distance form.
2. **Philosophical paragraph:** pending; the derived form `Ahyaliëo` and the full genitive structure require morphological verification.
3. **Literary context:** pending; grace and distance must remain distinct participants in the transformation rather than collapse into an opaque noun chain.

Verified source directions:

- `ahya-` “to change”: PM/395; the root is sourced, but the exact derived form `Ahyaliëo` is not directly attested by that citation.
- `cantië` “pattern”: NM/287–288; suitable for testing `code` as governing pattern rather than software code.
- `lissë` “grace”: VT44/18; a genitive `lisseo` is attested, but the chapter-specific sense still needs context testing.

Blocking source problem:

- the currently applied `Haiyava` is not supported by the recorded evidence;
- the sourced form `haia` is an adverb meaning “afar, far (off), far away,” not an attested noun “distance”;
- `haira` is an adjective meaning “remote, far”;
- `hairië` “distance, farness” is marked as a fan-invented Neo-Quenya form and cannot be adopted under the Late-Quenya-first profile without an explicit exception and independent review.

The earlier alternative `I Cantië yassë Lissë ar Haiya ahyar` is also deferred because it retains the unsupported `Haiya` form and its relative construction and plural agreement have not been independently verified.

State: `provisional-applied-audit-clear-revision-required`.
