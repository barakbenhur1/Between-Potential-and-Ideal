# Segment 020 clause test — round 2

Status: test only. Publication forbidden. Production source unchanged.

This file records the internally parsed round-2 test clauses after the adversarial review rejected the animate-pronoun route for Prime Intellect. It does not approve production wording or the segment.

## A. Directed speech to the person

`Prime Intellect quetë i coirëa quenen.`

Parse:
- `Prime Intellect`: subject, protected name.
- `quetë`: singular aorist of `quet-`.
- `i coirëa quenen`: dative recipient, “to the living person”.

Literal back-translation: Prime Intellect speaks to the living person.

Result: pass for controlled tests; final capitalization and protected-name policy remain production issues only.

## B. Directed speech back to the intelligence

Rejected first-pass form:

`I coirëa quén quetë sena.`

Reason for rejection: `sena` is attested as the long dative of animate `se`, but using it for Prime Intellect would silently classify the intelligence as animate. Segment 020 must keep the living person distinct from the intelligence and must not settle that ontological question through a pronoun.

Selected review notation:

`I coirëa quén quetë Prime Intellect-enna.`

Parse:
- `i coirëa quén`: subject, “the living person”.
- `quetë`: singular aorist of `quet-`.
- `Prime Intellect-enna`: protected proper name plus visible allative review suffix, “to Prime Intellect”.

Literal back-translation: The living person speaks to Prime Intellect.

Result: preferred controlled-test route; the hyphen is review notation and a final surface spelling is not approved.

## C. Agency and path

`I coirëa quén lelya tierya.`

Parse:
- `i coirëa quén`: subject, “the living person”.
- `lelya`: singular aorist of `lelya-`, “travels/proceeds”.
- `tie-rya`: path plus third-person singular possessive, written here as `tierya`.

Literal back-translation: The living person travels his/her path.

Contextual back-translation: The living participant travels their own path.

Result: pass for controlled tests; `tierya` is a documented Neo-Quenya formation and still requires external specialist review before production use.
