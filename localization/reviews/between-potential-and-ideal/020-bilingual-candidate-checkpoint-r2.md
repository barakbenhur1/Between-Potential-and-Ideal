# Segment 020 bilingual candidate checkpoint — round 2

Status: internal specialist checkpoint only. Production source unchanged. Publication forbidden.

This checkpoint compares the current Neo-Quenya and Klingon title candidates after their internal clause, context, and adversarial reviews. It does not select production wording and does not approve segment 020.

## Canonical semantic control

Both candidates must preserve the same source architecture:

1. the governing process changes Prime Intellect;
2. the living human participant remains the actor in their own life;
3. grace or mercy is expressed as restraint rather than takeover;
4. the intelligence stands beside the participant instead of replacing, possessing, or completing the participant's path;
5. no physical-distance term may substitute for the chapter's moral and relational distance.

## Neo-Quenya internal candidate

`I Cantië yassë Prime Intellect ahya`

`Lissë: I Coirëa Quén Lelya Tierya`

Controlled back-translation:

**The Pattern in which Prime Intellect Changes**

**Grace: The Living Participant Travels Their Own Path**

### Internal strengths

- `Cantië` retains the governing-pattern anchor.
- the verbal `ahya` route avoids unsupported `Ahyaliëo`.
- `coirëa quén` transparently identifies a living person rather than a literal water spring.
- `lelya tierya` keeps movement and the path with the living participant.
- the candidate avoids an abstract relation noun and a metaphysical remain verb.

### Remaining Neo-Quenya blockers

- PM/395 sense boundary and transitivity of `ahya-`;
- idiomatic acceptability of singular `yassë` in the title frame;
- external review of `tierya` as a documented but unattested whole formation;
- final styling of the protected name inside Neo-Quenya text;
- external independent specialist review.

## Klingon internal candidate

`Prime Intellect choHbogh mIw`

`pung: vang yInbogh ghot 'e' chaw' Prime Intellect`

Controlled back-translation:

**The Process that Changes Prime Intellect**

**Mercy: Prime Intellect Allows the Living Person to Act**

### Internal strengths

- the changing object and governing process are syntactically explicit.
- the former ambiguous `-meH` route is removed.
- the living person remains the grammatical subject of `vang`.
- the sentence-as-object construction makes the permitted event the person's action.
- no physical `He` path term, possession wording, or reciprocal `-chuq` route is used.

### Remaining Klingon blockers

- external confirmation that `mIw` can carry the intended governing philosophical-process sense rather than only a technical procedure;
- external confirmation that `pung` plus the explanatory clause preserves chosen restraint rather than pity;
- external confirmation of the sentence-as-object scope and stylistic acceptability;
- protected-name repetition and title styling;
- external independent Klingon specialist review.
