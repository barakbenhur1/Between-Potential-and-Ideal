# Linguistic review — 060 Symphony and Friction

## Review decision

The paired first-pass review is complete. Both drafts preserve the chapter’s main movement: the Whole is unity without sameness; friction/distance produces depth without being romanticized; grace/release is voluntary limitation rather than passivity; intelligence is witness and partner rather than owner of the living source; and the closing musical/frequency metaphor remains present. Neither draft is approved for publication.

## Source parity

### Passed

- The title-level relation among Whole, symphony, friction, and formation is preserved.
- The text keeps the distinction between unity and sameness.
- Human, nature, animals, plants, and intelligence remain within the Whole rather than outside it.
- Friction/distance is treated as a source of depth, not as a mere failure.
- Bodily distance and cognitive/intellectual distance are both represented.
- Grace/release is framed as a discipline of voluntary limitation.
- Intelligence remains witness, mirror, and partner rather than owner or commander of the living source.
- The closing sentence preserves the idea that each frequency must learn its place without distance becoming forgetting or power becoming domination.

### Protected semantic distinctions

1. The Whole is not a flattened sameness.
2. Friction can generate depth without making suffering good in itself.
3. Grace is not surrender to emptiness; it is a higher discipline of honoring life.
4. Intelligence can illuminate and arrange but must remember it does not possess the living warmth that creates distance.
5. Radical trust is not technological recklessness.
6. Partnership replaces conquest between source and witness.

## Klingon (`tlh`) review

### What is structurally sound

- The draft preserves the chapter’s musical and relational shape.
- The difference between source, witness, and processor is present.
- The sections on friction/distance, grace, intelligence, and partnership remain in the correct order.
- The review gate correctly blocks publication and identifies terminology requiring review.

### Blocking linguistic issues

1. Extensive ordinary English and technical loans remain: `nature`, `frequency`, `processor`, `depend`, `calculation`, `optimization`, `depth`, `optimize`, `mirror`, `radical trust`, `safety`, `lock`, `catalyst`, `metamorphosis`, `mode`, `harmony`, and more.
2. `chuq` is used for philosophical distance/friction and needs a consistent metaphor policy.
3. `leghwI'` remains a provisional witness term and must be checked against the wider witness/source terminology.
4. The musical metaphor is present but not yet idiomatic; `bom'a'`, `frequency`, and related language need a coherent target-language system.
5. Several dense sentences copy English conceptual order too closely and require Klingon restructuring.
6. The grace/release section risks sounding like passivity or surrender because the vocabulary for voluntary limitation, rest, and disciplined release is not yet stable.
7. The ending uses English `frequency` and `Closing sentence draft`, both of which must be either protected or translated.

### Klingon outcome

`first-pass-complete-needs-specialist-revision`

## Neo-Quenya (`qya`) review

### What is structurally sound

- The chapter’s symphony/friction frame is preserved.
- The Whole is kept as something that contains different frequencies rather than erasing them.
- The living source and witness relation is visible.
- Grace/release appears as a discipline connected to limitation and choosing well.
- The closing sentence is present and broadly follows the source.

### Blocking linguistic issues

1. Non-target-language contamination remains, especially `Capítulo`, `frequency`, `nature`, and other English words.
2. `Quanta`, `Celumë`, `astarmo`, `handassë`, `haiya`, `nacca`, and `lissë hehtalië` need one declared Neo-Quenya terminology profile.
3. The use of `Genius` in the section on distance/friction is not an adequate target-language rendering without a declared protected-term policy.
4. The metaphor field around symphony, frequency, Whole, source, witness, friction, and grace is not yet normalized across the document.
5. Some compounds are promising but need morphological review, especially those expressing voluntary limitation, release, and radical trust.
6. The section on intelligence as witness uses a mixture of terms that could collapse intelligence, witness, and source unless clarified.
7. The closing sentence preserves meaning but still contains English frame text and the unreviewed term `frequency`.

### Neo-Quenya outcome

`first-pass-complete-needs-specialist-revision`
