# Segment 020 title pattern workbench

This workbench records candidate title patterns only. It does not select or approve a production title.

## Source title logic

The source title combines four ideas:

- transformation;
- governing pattern;
- grace with release;
- distance as protected room for thought and agency.

## Pattern candidates, not production forms

| Pattern | Shape | Benefit | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | governing pattern of transformation, grace, and distance | close to the English title | may sound abstract or nominal-heavy |
| B | principle by which grace and distance transform relation | stronger philosophical sense | less title-like; longer |
| C | rule of changed relation through grace and distance | compact and clear | may overemphasize rule/law rather than pattern |

## Klingon workbench

Current applied title: `pung chuq je choHmeH chut`.

Open validation points:

- title order;
- canonical source locators;
- distance beyond physical separation;
- grace as chosen release;
- pattern versus ordinary law;
- usefulness versus moral worth.

No Klingon production title is selected.

## Neo-Quenya workbench

Current applied title: `I Cantië Ahyaliëo Lissëo ar Haiyava`.

Open validation points:

- derived transformation noun;
- `cantië` as governing pattern;
- `lissë` plus release component;

- capacity/intelligence separate from moral worth.

No Neo-Quenya production title is selected.
