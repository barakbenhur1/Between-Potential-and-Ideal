## Unresolved term: evidence

No single project form is approved for `evidence` in either language. Segment 260 deliberately distinguishes:

1. evidence supporting a philosophical or scientific claim;
2. a formal NP witness or certificate;
3. a proof or verification procedure;
4. living evidence carried by a source, experience, or testimony.

These senses must not be collapsed into one replacement. Each occurrence requires a context-specific descriptive rendering and specialist review. A mechanical global substitution is forbidden.
