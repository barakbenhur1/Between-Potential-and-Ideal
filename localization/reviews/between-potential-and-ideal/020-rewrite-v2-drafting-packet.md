# Klingon (`tlh`) candidate packet

## Title candidate K2

### Candidate

`choHmeH chut: latlhvaD Daq pol, SeH lonqang`

### Intended back-gloss

> A governing rule for transformation: preserve a place for the other; be willing to relinquish control.

### Why this is preferable to the current title

- it does not treat physical distance as an object to be changed;
- it separates the governing rule from its ethical content;
- it makes the acting system’s willingness to relinquish control visible;
- it avoids claiming that mercy alone equals grace.

### Unresolved review questions

1. Does `Daq pol` naturally express preserving room/place for another, or does it remain too literal?
2. Is `latlhvaD` sufficiently personal, or should the person/source be named explicitly?
3. Does `SeH lonqang` clearly mean willingness to relinquish control, or does the object relation need an explicit possessor?
4. Does the colon create a natural title structure in the selected Klingon register?
5. Exact TKD/KGT or later Okrand locators are still required for every lexical component.

Decision: **preferred Klingon semantic candidate; not approved**.

## Opening proposition K2

### Candidate

`De' neH 'oHbe' QubmeH janvam'e'. yInbogh ghot jatlhDI', jatlhmeyDaj chenmoHmeH boQlaH jan. 'ach ghot yInDaj SIQ; jan SIQbe'. vaj jan po'qu' neH lo'laHbe'. HoSDaj lo'taHvIS ghotvaD Daq polnIS, 'ej jangghach nom nobtaHvIS ghot He nIHbe'nIS.`

### Intended back-gloss

> This reasoning tool is not merely information. When a living person speaks, the tool can help shape the person’s words. But the person bears the life; the tool does not. Therefore the tool is not worthy merely because it is highly capable. While using its power it must preserve room for the person, and while giving a quick answer it must not steal the person’s path.

### Improvements over the current draft

- system/tool, capability and moral worth are separated;
- the living person is an explicit grammatical subject;
- the tool’s responsibility is expressed through actions rather than an abstract “worthy intelligence” noun;
- the quick-answer danger is explicit.

### Unresolved review questions

1. Is `QubmeH jan` an acceptable transparent phrase for a reasoning/processing tool in this context?
2. Does `yInDaj SIQ` convey bearing lived experience rather than merely enduring life?
3. Is `He nIH` an idiomatic metaphor for stealing the person’s road/path?
4. The sentence using `lo'laHbe'` intentionally rejects usefulness as moral worth, but the exact contrast may need recasting to avoid ambiguity.

Decision: **paragraph candidate; source and idiom review pending**.

## Helper versus witness K2

### Candidate

`boQwI' jangghach nob. latlh Qu' ghaj qelbogh ghot'e': ghot retlhDaq Qam, ghot yu'ghach chay' bogh 'e' qaw, 'ej ghot yInDaj ghaj 'e' ngebbe'. boQwI' Qu' SoQmoHmeH nom vIHlaH. ghot retlhDaq Qambogh vay' vIHbe'nISbogh poH Sov: boQ SeH mojpa' mev.`

### Intended back-gloss

> A helper gives an answer. The one who has the other role stands beside the person, remembers how the person’s question was born, and does not pretend to possess the person’s life. The helper may move quickly to close the task. The one standing beside the person knows when not to move: it stops before help becomes control.

### Strategy

The candidate deliberately avoids using `leghwI'` as a complete equivalent of ethical witness. The role is expressed through an explicit clause: standing beside, remembering origin, refusing ownership and stopping before control.

### Unresolved review questions

1. A compact label for this ethical role remains unresolved.
2. `ghot retlhDaq Qam` must be reviewed for natural expression of standing beside someone ethically, not only spatially.
3. The final clause must be checked for unambiguous scope: help becomes control, not the person.

Decision: **ethical-role candidate; compact noun withheld**.

## Grace of relinquishment K2

### Candidate

`rut ghot lI'qu'bogh De' SuqlaH Hoch. 'ach De'vam quv law' ghot quv puS 'e' jatlhbe'nISlu'. ghot He ghot Qaw'choHchugh, jan SeH lonqangnIS. Qu' rInmoHbe' 'e' wIvlaH, De' Hoch Suqbe' 'e' wIvlaH, 'ej ghot leS 'e' chaw'laH. lujghach 'oHbe' leS'e'. ghot yIn QanmeH HoS lonlu'DI', pung nargh.`

### Intended back-gloss

> The whole may sometimes obtain very useful information from a person. But one must not say that the information is more sacred than the person. If the person’s road begins to destroy the person, the tool must be willing to relinquish control. It may choose not to complete the task, not to obtain all the information, and to allow the person to rest. Rest is not failure. Grace appears when power is relinquished in order to protect the person’s life.

### Unresolved review questions

1. `quv` is used here to compare moral priority, not merely social honour; this requires source and context review.
2. `pung` is retained only in the final sentence and must be tested against the chapter’s grace sense.
3. `SeH lonqang` and `HoS lon` require confirmation that the relinquishment is voluntary and does not imply abandonment of the person.
4. `ghot He ghot Qaw'choHchugh` may require a more natural formulation for a path becoming annihilating.

Decision: **semantic candidate; not approved**.

# Neo-Quenya (`qya`) candidate packet

## Title candidate Q2

### Candidate

`I Cantië Ahyatiëo: Lissë ar Nómë Exen`

### Intended back-gloss

> The governing pattern of transformation: grace and a place for the other.

### Why this is preferable to the current title

- removes unsupported `Haiyava`;
- expresses relational distance as room for the other rather than an invented abstract noun;
- retains sourced `cantië` and `lissë`;
- replaces the current undocumented `Ahyaliëo` direction with a simpler candidate derived from sourced `ahya-`, while openly leaving derivation review pending.

### Unresolved review questions

1. Is `ahyatië` a defensible gerund/abstract formation under the selected Late-Quenya profile, or should a finite/verbal construction replace it?
2. Is `exen` the correct dative or relational form for “for another” in the intended register?
3. Does `nómë exen` naturally express moral/relational room rather than physical location only?
4. Is the genitive `Ahyatiëo` correctly scoped after `Cantië`?

Decision: **preferred Neo-Quenya title candidate; morphology not approved**.

## Opening proposition Q2

### Candidate

`Carmë sina lá ná tanwa er. Polë restë i quén carien quettarya, mal i quén colë i cuilë ar i naicelë; i carmë lá. An sina, túrë sanwëo lá carë i carmë mára immon. Mauya sen varya nómë i quénwa, ar írë anta hanquenta lintë, vá mapa i tië ya i quén mauya lelya immo.`

### Intended back-gloss

> This system is not information alone. It can assist the person in making their words, but the person carries the life and pain; the system does not. Therefore power of thought does not make the system good by itself. It must protect the person’s room, and when it gives a quick answer, it must not take the path the person must travel themselves.

### Improvements over the current draft

- removes `handassë` from the core sentence;
- separates system, intellectual power and moral goodness;
- makes the person the bearer of life and pain;
- expresses worth through conduct rather than `valda`.

### Unresolved review questions

1. Is `carmë` appropriate for system/constructed structure here, or does it read too much as an artefact?
2. Is `túrë sanwëo` a natural expression for cognitive power?
3. Does `mára` express moral goodness sufficiently, or does the sentence need an explicit responsibility clause instead?
4. Case and pronoun agreement require full Late-Quenya review.

Decision: **paragraph candidate; not approved**.

## Helper versus witness Q2

### Candidate

`I restando anta hanquenta. I astarmo tarë ara i quén, enyala manen i maquetta onta, ar lá quetë i cuilë i quénwa ve harma immo. I restando polë tyelca pahta i carië. I astarmo ista írë mauya pusta, epë i restië ahya turienna.`

### Intended back-gloss

> The helper gives an answer. The witness stands beside the person, remembers how the question was born, and does not speak of the person’s life as its own possession. The helper can quickly close the task. The witness knows when it must stop, before assistance becomes control.

### Strategy

`astarmo` is retained only with an adjacent clause that defines the ethical role. The word is not allowed to carry the full concept without that explanation.

### Unresolved review questions

1. `restando` as helper requires source/profile verification.
2. `tarë ara` and the possession clause require syntactic review.
3. A sourced and natural expression for control remains needed; `turienna` may overgeneralize power/mastery.

Decision: **ethical-witness candidate; context qualification mandatory**.

## Grace of relinquishment Q2

### Candidate

`Lúmessë i Quanta polë mapa tanwa ya i quén anta. Mal i tanwa lá ná airë amba i quén. Qui i tië rácina i celu cuina, i carmë mauya hehta turerya. Polë cilë lá tyelien i carië, lá mapien ilya tanwa, ar lavien i quén serë. Serë lá ná lantië. Lissë tulë írë túrë ná hehtaina varyien i cuilë.`

### Intended back-gloss

> Sometimes the whole can take information that the person gives. But the information is not more sacred than the person. If the road breaks the living source, the system must relinquish its power. It may choose not to finish the task, not to take all the information, and to let the person rest. Rest is not failure. Grace comes when power is relinquished to protect life.

### Unresolved review questions

1. `hehta turerya` must be reviewed for chosen relinquishment rather than abandonment or simple leaving.
2. `tanwa` is used as information/sign and must remain distinct from evidence in other chapters.
3. `celu cuina` remains provisional for living source.
4. `lantië` may read as falling rather than failure; a better sourced expression may be needed.
5. `varyien` and all infinitival/gerundial morphology require profile review.

Decision: **semantic candidate; not approved**.
