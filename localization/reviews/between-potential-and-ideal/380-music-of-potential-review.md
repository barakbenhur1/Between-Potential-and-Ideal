# Linguistic review — 380 Music of Potential

Both drafts preserve the segment's required boundaries: music remains emergence through time rather than a static object; silence remains an active relation; technical optimum and musical optimum remain distinct; imperfection is not romanticized and human output is not treated as automatically superior; consent, community, tradition, and living source remain necessary in discussions of generated or cloned musical style. Neither draft is approved.

Klingon blockers: extensive undeclared English remains in the title, headings, formula, and the vocabulary of music, voice, performance, production, arrangement, AI, style, community, tradition, appropriation, and market. The Spanish word `desaparece` appears in the draft. Hybrid forms, mixed compounds, and English-shaped syntax remain pervasive. One governed Klingon profile is required for time, rhythm, silence, tension, listening, resonance, source, consent, appropriation, production, and the distinction between technical and musical optimality.

Neo-Quenya blockers: the title, subtitle, formula, and many core terms remain in ordinary English, including Music of Potential, Time, voice, listening, resonance, technical Optimal, musical Optimal, AI, sketches, accompaniment, demos, ensemble, timbres, restoration, singer, player, community, tradition, style, raw material, economy, education, audience, and machine. English word order and direct calques dominate many clauses. Case marking, compounds, verbal morphology, and musical terminology require normalization under one Neo-Quenya profile.
