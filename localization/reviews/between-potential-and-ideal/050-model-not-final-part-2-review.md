# Linguistic review — 050 Model, Not Final Declaration, Part 2

## Review decision

The paired first-pass review is complete. Both drafts preserve the chapter’s stopping conditions, the priority of the living source over the model, the distinction between metaphor and proof, the criticism/drainage function, and AI’s limited role as mirror and critic. Neither draft is ready for approval. Both require specialist recasting under stable terminology and language profiles.

## Source parity

### Passed

- Metaphor is not treated as proof.
- Scientific vocabulary is not allowed to grant borrowed authority to metaphysical claims.
- The framework must be able to stop, qualify, revise, reject, or admit missing sources.
- The living source precedes the model and cannot be reduced to extracted information.
- Testimony remains more than data and carries distance, cost, embodiment, memory, and vulnerability.
- AI remains a tool for criticism and comparison, not an authority, living source, or substitute for judgment.
- A framework that can explain every possible outcome is presented as unfalsifiable and therefore defective.
- Hope remains without guarantee, and nihilism is not allowed to become an excuse for abandoning responsibility.
- Courage and humility remain the chapter’s paired fidelity requirements.

### Protected semantic distinctions

1. A metaphor may illuminate a relation without proving the reality to which it points.
2. A scientific term must be marked as scientific, metaphorical, structural, or inspirational each time its role could be confused.
3. Stopping conditions are part of truthfulness, not weakness.
4. Testimony may inform a model but must not become the model’s prisoner.
5. A responsible model asks not only what it may learn from testimony but what it must not take from it.
6. AI may increase fluency while decreasing discipline; smoothness is not reliability.
7. Revision, limitation, and rejection are signs that a theory remains alive.
8. Hope must not conceal uncertainty, and criticism must not collapse into despair.

## Klingon (`tlh`) review

### What is structurally sound

- Every numbered section from metaphor and science through stopping conditions, living source, AI, unfalsifiability, nihilism with hope, and the conclusion is present.
- The hierarchy of living source, testimony, model, and extracted data remains intact.
- The AI limitation is explicit and does not grant AI ownership of judgment or lived experience.
- The chapter’s stopping language is repeated consistently at the conceptual level.

### Blocking linguistic issues

1. The draft contains pervasive ungoverned English loans and code-switching, including ordinary prose as well as technical labels.
2. The loans are not consistently marked as protected technical labels, quotations, or translation gaps.
3. `pat` continues to cover model, framework, and system without a controlled distinction, while the glossary proposes `nab` or descriptive representation language for “model.”
4. `Qu'` continues to carry task, discipline, responsibility, and duty, which obscures ethical differences central to the chapter.
5. Long English-shaped clause chains and stacked nominalizations reduce Klingon readability and require syntactic reconstruction.
6. Terms for metaphor, proof, testimony, evidence, model, source, stopping condition, criticism, revision, and falsifiability remain unapproved.
7. The criticism/drainage image is structurally preserved but still relies on mixed-language expressions rather than a stable Klingon formulation.
8. Technical named terms may remain protected only under a declared policy; ordinary English connective and evaluative vocabulary must be replaced.

## Neo-Quenya (`qya`) review

### What is structurally sound

- The complete numbered sequence and conclusion are preserved.
- The living-source/testimony/model hierarchy remains visible.
- AI remains a mirror and testing instrument rather than a source of authority.
- The model’s ability to stop and fail is explicitly retained.
- Hope and nihilism remain in productive tension rather than being merged.

### Blocking linguistic issues

1. Confirmed language contamination remains: Spanish `metafísica` and `Capítulo`; ordinary English items also remain throughout the draft.
2. These forms are not consistently declared as protected modern technical vocabulary.
3. `patar` again carries model, framework, and system, conflicting with the working glossary direction toward `emma` or a transparent descriptive phrase.
4. `emma` is simultaneously used for representation, metaphor, and model-like functions, so the conceptual distinctions are unstable.
5. `astarmë` is used for testimony or witnessing while the glossary points toward `cenindo` for observer/witness; the ethical and evidentiary roles require separate approved forms.
6. Project formations such as `mauya-cárië`, `lissë hehtalië`, `sanwë-patar`, and several compound abstractions remain unapproved.
7. Agreement, case endings, participles, compounds, and English-influenced word order require a single declared Neo-Quenya profile and full idiomatic rewrite.
8. The final section title and repeated use of `Capítulo` must be normalized consistently with segment 040.
9. Scientific and computational labels may remain protected only where necessary and visibly governed; ordinary foreign-language prose cannot remain.
