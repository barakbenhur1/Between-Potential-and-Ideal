# Linguistic review — 050 Model, Not Final Declaration — Part 2

## Review decision

The paired first-pass review is complete. Both drafts preserve the second half of the chapter’s architecture: scientific and metaphysical language must remain bounded; the system must be able to stop; the living source precedes the model; testimony is not reducible to extracted data; AI can serve as mirror and critic but not authority or living owner; and the ending holds hope without converting it into certainty. Neither draft is publication-ready.

## Source parity

### Passed

- The draft preserves the distinction between metaphor, scientific proof, measurement, theorem, and inspiration.
- The warning against turning physics, cosmology, quantum language, or metaphysics into borrowed authority remains visible.
- The explicit stopping conditions remain present.
- The living source is kept prior to the model.
- Testimony is treated as more than data and as arriving from a living source.
- The AI section keeps both sides: AI as useful analytic tool and AI as risk of polished overclaim.
- The criticism/drainage function is preserved as a corrective metaphor rather than a formal scientific mechanism.
- The hope-versus-nihilism ending remains balanced: no guaranteed redemption, but also no surrender to despair.

### Protected semantic distinctions

1. A metaphor can illuminate without proving.
2. Scientific vocabulary must not be used as aura, borrowed authority, or disguised proof.
3. A responsible system must be able to say where it is metaphor, value claim, weak comparison, or possible harm.
4. A living source cannot become raw material for the model’s cleverness.
5. Testimony carries distance, cost, body, memory, and vulnerability.
6. AI can reveal inconsistencies and exaggeration but cannot replace living judgment.
7. A theory’s repairability is a sign of life, not a weakness.
8. Hope cannot become certainty, and nihilism cannot become an excuse to abandon responsibility.

## Klingon (`tlh`) review

### What is structurally sound

- The sequence of sections 5–11 is preserved.
- The warnings about borrowed scientific authority and metaphysical camouflage are present.
- The stopping-conditions section is clear in purpose.
- The living-source and testimony sections preserve the intended ethical hierarchy.
- The AI section correctly frames AI as tool, mirror, and risk rather than authority.
- The final balance of courage and humility is preserved.

### Blocking linguistic issues

1. Ordinary English and ungoverned technical loans remain throughout: `energy`, `weight`, `field`, `medium`, `horizon`, `information`, `system`, `recursion`, `boundary`, `experiment`, `theorem`, `measurement`, `metaphysics`, `lab coat`, `Overclaim`, `value claim`, `weak comparison`, `AI`, `mirror`, `reject`, and others.
2. `pat` continues to carry model, framework, theory, and system. This must be disambiguated before approval.
3. `Qu'` continues to cover task, discipline, duty, and responsibility, which weakens the moral-responsibility argument.
4. The section on physics as not a warehouse of symbols for metaphysics is semantically present but needs idiomatic restructuring and a stable vocabulary for physical science, metaphor, proof, and borrowed authority.
5. The testimony section uses `testimony De'` and related mixed expressions that risk reducing testimony to information despite the source’s opposite claim.
6. The AI section has several English insertions and English-shaped lists, especially around continuity, contradiction, style, polish, and overclaim.
7. The hope/nihilism passage preserves the logic but uses heavy negation chains that require specialist checking for Klingon scope and readability.

## Neo-Quenya (`qya`) review

### What is structurally sound

- The contrast between metaphor and proof is preserved.
- The need for stopping conditions is present.
- The living source remains prior to the model.
- The AI passage preserves both usefulness and danger.
- The ending keeps hope open without converting it to certainty.
- The review gate remains explicit and publication is forbidden.

### Blocking linguistic issues

1. Non-target-language contamination remains: `metafísica`, `energy`, `weight`, `field`, `medium`, `horizon`, `information`, `system`, `recursion`, `boundary`, `theorem`, `quantum theory`, `contradiction`, `style`, `consistency`, `compare versions`, `nihilism`, and `Capítulo`.
2. `patar`, `emma`, and `tanwa` overlap in the model/metaphor/representation/proof field and need a profile-wide terminology decision.
3. `Curwaina Handassë` is an understandable draft for AI but needs project-wide approval and consistency with all other AI references.
4. The testimony/living-source passage uses `astarmë`, `celu cuina`, `haiya`, `valë`, and `aucassë`; these need consistent definitions so that testimony does not become mere counting or detached report.
5. `lissë hehtaliëo` for graceful relinquishment and similar compounds are promising but must be normalized morphologically.
6. The phrase corresponding to criticism/drainage/outlet is present but still too metaphorically loose; it needs to preserve critique as an internal safety mechanism without becoming plumbing imagery alone.
7. Several sentences preserve source order too directly and need smoother Neo-Quenya syntax.
