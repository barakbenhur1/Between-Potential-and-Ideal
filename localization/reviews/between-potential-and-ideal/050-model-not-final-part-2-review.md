# Linguistic review — 050 Model, Not Final Declaration, Part 2

## Decision

Both drafts preserve the stopping conditions, the priority of the living source over the model, metaphor/proof separation, criticism as a corrective mechanism, AI as mirror rather than authority, and the tension between hope and nihilism. Neither draft is approved.

## Klingon blockers

- Pervasive ordinary English and ungoverned technical loans remain.
- `pat` still collapses model, framework, and system.
- `Qu'` still collapses task, discipline, duty, and responsibility.
- Terms for metaphor, proof, testimony, evidence, stopping condition, revision, and falsifiability are uncontrolled.
- Clause chains and nominalizations require full idiomatic recasting.

Outcome: `first-pass-complete-needs-specialist-revision`.

## Neo-Quenya blockers

- Spanish `metafísica` and `Capítulo`, plus ordinary English, remain.
- `patar` and `emma` cover overlapping model/framework/representation/metaphor functions.
- `astarmë` conflicts with the current witness/testimony direction.
- Project compounds and morphology require one declared Neo-Quenya profile and full rewrite.

Outcome: `first-pass-complete-needs-specialist-revision`.

Keep both sources as `draft` with `publication: forbidden`.
