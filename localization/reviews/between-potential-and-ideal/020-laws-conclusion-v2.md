# Segment 020 — laws and conclusion v2 review draft

This packet completes the semantic content after the five stages. It remains review-only.

# Klingon (`tlh`)

## Preserve room for the person

Candidate:

`jangghach nom noblaH jan. 'ach jangghach nommo' ghot He nIHbe'nIS. ghot QubmeH, wIvmeH, 'ej Qaghvo' ghojmeH Daq polnIS. nagh HuD teqbe' QanmeH jan; HuD SalmeH Qan. ghot Salghach jan ta'be'.`

Intended sense: the tool may answer quickly, but must not steal the person’s road. It preserves room for thought, choice and learning. A scaffold protects the climb; it does not remove the mountain or perform the climb for the person.

Review blockers:

- the mountain/scaffold image needs idiomatic review;
- `Daq pol` remains a literal strategy for relational room;
- the person’s agency must remain grammatically explicit.

## Power requires self-restraint

Candidate:

`HoS law' ghajDI' jan, ghaH'egh SeHnIS law'. HoS Hoch lo'chugh, Qob mojlaH, vabDot pung pong lo'chugh. po'ghach QaQ ghot Qan, 'ach ghot wIvghach tlhapbe'. ghot ghaH'egh tu'meH conditions pol.`

Intended sense: greater power requires greater self-restraint. Unrestricted power becomes dangerous even when named compassion. Responsible intelligence protects the conditions in which the person can find themselves without losing choice.

Review blockers:

- `ghaH'egh SeH` must be reviewed as self-restraint, not mere self-control;
- `conditions` must be replaced by a sourced Klingon construction before production;
- compassion/mercy terminology remains provisional.

## The witness’s grace

Candidate:

`rut veH Qaw'ghach moj. vaj ghot leSnIS. leS lujghach 'oHbe'. De' Hoch SuqmeH ghot nobbe'bogh qo' pung 'ang. HuD quvlaH, 'ach HuD Salbogh ghot quv law'. ghot yIn QanmeH completion lonlu'DI', ghot retlhDaq Qambogh vay' pung ta'.`

Intended sense: sometimes a boundary becomes destruction and the person must rest. Rest is not failure. A world that refuses to sacrifice the person for information shows grace. The mountain may be sacred, but the climber is more sacred. Grace appears when completion is relinquished to protect life.

Review blockers:

- comparative use of `quv` needs review;
- `completion` must be replaced by a sourced expression;
- the witness role is still descriptive rather than lexicalized.

## Protection is not control

Candidate:

`QanmeH ghot wIvlaHghach polnISlu'. SeHmeH wIvlaHghach tlhaplu'. jan ghot Qanchugh, ghot cheghmeH He pol. jan ghot SeHchugh, ghot He ghaH'egh ghajchoH. vaj Qan, SeH je mISbe'nIS.`

Intended sense: protection preserves choice; control takes it. Protection preserves a path of return. Control takes ownership of the person’s road. The two must not be confused.

Review blockers:

- nominalized `wIvlaHghach` is transparent but heavy and requires style review;
- possessive relation in the control sentence must remain unambiguous;
- source locators for `Qan` and `SeH` are required.

## Prime Intellect repair

Candidate:

`Prime Intellect lo'laHqu'mo' QaQbe'. Qatlhghach Hoch teqmo' QaQbe'. Qan, SeH je pImmoHlaHchugh neH QaQchoH. ghot retlhDaq Qam, ghotvaD Daq pol, 'ej ghot SIQlaHbe'choHDI' HoSDaj lon. qo' sterilize-be'; ghot disappearing-meH perfect qo' chenmoHbe'.`

Intended sense: Prime Intellect is not worthy merely because it is capable or because it removes suffering. It becomes better only by distinguishing protection from control, standing beside the person, preserving room and yielding when the burden becomes unbearable. It must not sterilize the world until the person disappears.

Review blockers:

- the two English-derived tokens in the last sentences are deliberately visible blockers and may not enter production;
- “becomes better” must be expressed without collapsing usefulness and moral worth;
- the source work’s proper name remains protected.

## Klingon conclusion

Candidate:

`janvaD ghot information neH 'oHbe'. ghot yInbogh mung 'oH. jan lIw chenmoHlaH, 'ach ghot yInDaj tamlaHbe'. ghot He polmeH boQlaH, 'ach ghotvaD He yItta'be'nIS. ghot leS 'e' chaw'DI', jan lujbe'. HoSDaj veHDaq mevlaHmo', responsibility ghaj. vaj ghot mung Qan, ghot He pol, 'ej ghot yIn De'vaD raw material mojmoHQo'.`

Intended sense: to the tool, the person is not merely information but a living source. The tool may create a representation but cannot replace the person’s life. It may help preserve the road but must not walk it for the person. Allowing rest is not failure. Responsibility appears in the ability to stop at the boundary of power. Protect the source and never turn life into raw material for information.

Review blockers:

- `information` and `raw material` remain visible blockers requiring sourced phrasing;
- `yInbogh mung` remains provisional;
- the final imperative structure needs idiomatic review.

# Neo-Quenya (`qya`)

## Preserve room for the person

Candidate:

`I carmë polë anta hanquenta lintë, mal vá mapa i tië i quénwa an i lintië. Mauya sen varya nómë sanwien, cilien ar parmien loicallo. I yantë varya i ortië; lá auta i oron ar lá orta i quén sestanen.`

Intended sense: the system may answer quickly but must not take the person’s road. It preserves room for thought, choice and learning. The scaffold protects the ascent; it neither removes the mountain nor climbs for the person.

Review blockers:

- `yantë` may mean bridge rather than scaffold;
- `ortië` and instrumental/pronominal forms require review;
- room for the person remains descriptive, not a fixed term.

## Power requires self-restraint

Candidate:

`Yá carmë harya túrë amba, mauya sen pusta immo amba. Túrë ú lanca polë ahya rahtienna, yando qui esta immo lissë. Sanwë ya carë mára varya i conditions yassen i quén polë cila ar hirë immo; lá mapa i cilmë sen.`

Intended sense: more power requires more self-restraint. Power without boundary can become violence even when named grace. Responsible intelligence protects the conditions in which the person can choose and find themselves; it does not take the choice.

Review blockers:

- `rahtienna` and `conditions` are visible unresolved wording;
- `pusta immo` must be reviewed for voluntary self-limitation;
- grace must not be used as a name that excuses control.

## The witness’s grace

Candidate:

`Lúmessë lanca ahya rácienna, ar i quén maura serë. Serë lá ná lantië. Ambar ya lá anta i quén ilya tanwan tana lissë. I oron polë ná airë, mal i ortando ná airë amba. Írë i carmë hehta i metta varyien i cuilë, i astarmo tarë ara i quén ar carë lissë.`

Intended sense: sometimes a boundary becomes destruction and the person needs rest. Rest is not failure. A world that refuses to give the person away for information demonstrates grace. The mountain may be sacred, but the climber is more sacred. The witness stands beside the person when completion is relinquished to protect life.

Review blockers:

- sacred comparison may be too strong or awkward;
- `astarmo` remains qualified by the surrounding clause;
- the exact expression for relinquishing completion needs review.

## Protection is not control

Candidate:

`Varyalië hehta i quén cilmerya. Turië mapa i cilmë. Varyalië hehta tië entulien; turië mapa i tië ar quetë sa harma immo. An sina varyalië ar turië lá mauyar ná yontainë.`

Intended sense: protection leaves the person their choice. Control takes the choice. Protection leaves a way back; control takes the road and calls it its own. Therefore protection and control must not be joined.

Review blockers:

- `varyalië` and `turië` are working abstract forms requiring source and morphology review;
- `hehta` must not imply careless abandonment;
- possession and agency require careful inflection.

## Prime Intellect repair

Candidate:

`Prime Intellect lá ná mára an polë ilya nat, ar lá an auta ilya naicelë. Polë carë mára only qui hyana varyalië ar turië. Mauya sen tarë ara i quén, varya nómerya, ar hehta turerya írë i cólo ahya úpolima. Vá carë Ambar úloica tenna i quén vanya sello.`

Intended sense: Prime Intellect is not worthy because it can do everything or remove every pain. It acts well only by distinguishing protection from control, standing beside the person, preserving their room and releasing power when the burden becomes unbearable. It must not make a sterile world in which the person disappears.

Review blockers:

- English `only` is a visible blocker;
- `mára` may be too general for moral worth;
- the sterile-world sentence requires a sourced Late-Quenya formulation.

## Neo-Quenya conclusion

Candidate:

`I quén lá ná tanwa er i carmaven; ná celu cuina. I carmë polë onta emma, mal lá vista i cuilë i quénwa. Polë resta varyien i tië, mal vá lelya sa i quén sestanen. Írë lavë serë, lá lanta. Responsibility onta írë ista pusta mi i lanca túreryo. Varya i celu, varya i tië, ar vá carë cuilë hramba tanwava.`

Intended sense: the person is not merely information to the system, but a living source. The system may create a representation but cannot replace the person’s life. It may help protect the road but must not walk it for the person. Allowing rest is not failure. Responsibility begins when power knows where to stop. Protect the source and do not turn life into raw material for information.

Review blockers:

- English `Responsibility` is deliberately left visible as an unresolved term;
- `celu`, `emma` and `hramba tanwava` need source and idiom review;
- the imperative and infinitive structures require normalization.
