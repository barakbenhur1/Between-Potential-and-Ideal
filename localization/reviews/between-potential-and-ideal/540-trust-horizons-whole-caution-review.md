# Linguistic review — 540 Trust Horizons, Frozen Identity, and the Whole

Both drafts preserve the required cautions: event horizons of trust remain conceptual metaphors rather than moral or social physics; a person remains distinct from the relation through which new information can no longer pass; all domain examples remain present—betrayal, law after sentence, economic credit, digital archives, groups, institutions, art, and language; refusal can remain protective, and repair does not promise restored trust or return to the outside; God remains one cautious possible name for the Whole rather than a particle mover, miracle operator, physical force, black-hole punishment, or white-hole body; scientific examples remain mirrors rather than proofs; Ideal remains an appearance that does not betray the Potential from which it came. Neither draft is approved.

Klingon blockers: extensive undeclared English remains in the title, headings, conceptual map, and vocabulary of trust, event horizon, interpretation, identity, betrayal, legal sentence, credit, digital archive, algorithm, institution, public relations, investigation, repair, redemption, God, Whole, source, mirror, and justification. Hybrid constructions and English-shaped syntax remain pervasive. A governed Klingon profile is required for interpretive blockage, historical trust, archived identity, protective refusal, non-redemptive repair, theological caution, scientific metaphor, and source fidelity.

Neo-Quenya blockers: the title, headings, conceptual map, and nearly all trust, digital, institutional, and theological terminology remain in ordinary English. English word order and mixed clauses dominate the discussion of betrayal, legal completion, credit, archives, algorithms, institutions, art, language, repair, God, Whole, mirrors, and justification.
