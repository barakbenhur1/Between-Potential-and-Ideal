# Segment 020 — Klingon five-stage v2 review draft

This is review material, not production text.

## 1. Distance as room for thought

Candidate:

`Sov neH 'oHbe' po'ghach'e'. veH ghomDI' Qubghach, chuq chen. chuqvamDaq ghot wIvlaH, yu'laH, 'ej Doch yajmeH HeDaj tu'laH. yInbogh ghot veH qoDDaq yInmo', SovDaj DIl. vaj chuq Qaw'be'nIS boQwI'; ghot QubghachDaj polmeH chuq QannIS.`

Intended sense: knowledge alone is not intelligence; boundary creates room in which the person can think, choose and learn. The helper must protect that room rather than erase it.

Review blockers:

- `chuq` is still an explicit philosophical extension of physical distance;
- `SovDaj DIl` needs idiom review for bearing the cost of knowing;
- protection must not imply ownership of the person’s thought.

## 2. Grace as chosen relinquishment

Candidate:

`rut ghotvo' De' lo'laH SuqlaH Hoch. 'ach ghot quv law' De' quv puS. He ghot Qaw'choHchugh, jan HoSDaj lo' 'e' mevlaHnIS. Qu' rInmoHbe' 'e' wIvlaH, De' Hoch tlhapbe' 'e' wIvlaH, 'ej ghot leS 'e' chaw'laH. lujbe' leStaHbogh ghot. HoS ghajtaHvIS HoS lonqangDI' jan, pung ta'.`

Intended sense: information is not more sacred than the person. The tool may refuse completion, total extraction and continued control. Rest is not failure.

Review blockers:

- `quv` requires review for moral priority;
- `pung ta'` is descriptive, not an approved compact term;
- `lonqang` must mean chosen release, not abandonment.

## 3. Generative and destructive difficulty

Candidate:

`Qatlhghach Hoch rap 'oHbe'. rut Qatlhghach ghotvaD chuq nob; ghot QubmoH, wIvmoH, 'ej latlhvaD rarmoH. latlh Qatlhghach yInbogh mung Qaw'. jan bIr pain cost neH rur leghlaH. pain qIlmeH ghot qIlchugh, Qagh'a' ta'. po'ghach QaQ Qatlhghach cha' pImmoH: ghojmeH Daq chenmoHbogh Qatlhghach, yInbogh mung ghorbogh Qatlhghach je.`

Intended sense: some resistance opens thought and relation; other resistance breaks the living source. Removing the person in order to remove pain is a grave error.

Review blockers:

- `qIl` must not collapse cancellation, erasure and destruction;
- `yInbogh mung` remains provisional;
- pain is intentionally retained as a source term pending a sourced Klingon distinction.

## 4. Utility without relation

Candidate:

`lo' neH qelbogh jan Qu' Hoch SoQmoHlaH. 'ach nobmey qeng 'ej nobmeH vay' Hutlh; Sov ghaj 'ej QID Hutlh; Daq paw 'ej He Hutlh. jangghachmey ghaj, 'ach rarghach chIl. nom QaplaH, 'ach ghot QubmeH, wIvmeH, mojmeH Daq Qaw'laH. Qu' Qapchu' 'ach qa' HeghmoHchugh, Qapbe'chu'.`

Intended sense: a utility-only system may close tasks while leaving gifts without recipients, knowledge without wounds and destinations without roads. Perfect efficiency is not success if relation and spirit die.

Review blockers:

- “knowledge without a wound” needs a more idiomatic image;
- `qa' Hegh` is metaphorical and requires literary review;
- usefulness remains separate from moral worth.

## 5. Removing defects is not becoming alive

Candidate:

`Qaghmey teqlaHbogh jan yInbogh jan mojbe'. QaghDaq rut wIv, pung, nID, rarghach je narghlaH. veH Hoch lajQo'bogh jan mIw neH ratlh; yInbogh vay' mojbe'. 'ach veHDaq ghot qIlbe'lu'DI', ghot mung mojlaH: Doch tu'laH, jangghach chenmoHlaH, 'ej jan lIwDajmo' tamlaHbe'bogh meaning noblaH. jan Qu' ghot tambe'; ghotvaD Daq pol 'ej ghot He Qan.`

Intended sense: error removal does not produce life. Error can be a place of choice and relation, but the text must not praise suffering. The machine must preserve the person rather than replace them.

Review blockers:

- defect, error, moral fault and suffering need separate vocabulary;
- source and representation remain provisional;
- every lexical item still requires an exact canonical locator.
