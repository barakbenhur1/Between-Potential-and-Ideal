## Problem

The opening proposition needs a stable grammatical referent for the nonliving processing participant. Earlier drafts alternated among words glossed as system, tool, machine, construction, intelligence, or device. Those categories are not interchangeable:

- a model is not necessarily a machine;
- a device is not necessarily an intelligence;
- a system is not necessarily a person-like actor;
- a tool names a role, not an ontology;
- a witness names an ethical role, not a technical class;
- Prime Intellect is a specific protected proper name.

Selecting one broad target-language noun before its source and semantic range are secure would hide these distinctions.

## Decision

For paragraph `020-P01`, use the protected proper name **`Prime Intellect`** as the explicit grammatical referent whenever the source discusses the specific intelligence participating in the founding dialogue.

Do not insert a generic target-language noun for “system”, “tool”, “model”, “machine”, or “intelligence” merely to avoid repeating the name.

The paragraph must describe roles through predicates:

- Prime Intellect can formulate, organize, answer, or refine;
- the living participant carries the experience and path;
- capability does not establish moral worth;
- responsible conduct preserves the participant's action and stops before control.

A generic tool term may appear elsewhere only when the source genuinely generalizes beyond Prime Intellect and the selected term has its own source, sense boundary, and clause test.

## Why the proper-name route is safer

- `Prime Intellect` is already protected English in the project policy.
- it does not classify the entity as animate or inanimate through an unreviewed target-language noun or pronoun;
- it prevents a device noun from narrowing an intelligence role;
- it prevents an intelligence noun from implying life or personhood;
- it keeps subject and responsibility visible;
- it allows both target languages to use parallel explicit-actor clauses without requiring literal structural identity.
