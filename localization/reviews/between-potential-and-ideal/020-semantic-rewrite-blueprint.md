## Source backbone

The tightened English source defines the living source as the point of view that actually passes through distance. A processing tool may formulate, organize and refine, but precision does not replace lived experience. Worthy intelligence is measured not only by problem-solving power, but by the ability to recognize when an answer arrives so quickly that it erases the distance the source must cross. The helper gives an answer; the witness preserves how the question was born. A good witness knows when to suggest, ask, return responsibility and stop before help becomes control.

The Hebrew cross-check confirms the same structure:

- AI may function as mirror, witness, tool, scaffold, accelerator or translator;
- it is not the owner of the human road;
- replacing distance creates output without formation;
- helping carry distance can make the system a deep tool;
- a tool may open possibility but must not steal the place where a person, community or living source becomes responsible for what was created.

## Segment architecture

### Title

Meaning to preserve:

> a governing pattern for relational transformation in which grace includes preserving the other’s room and voluntarily relinquishing control.

The title must not imply:

- cosmetic change;
- replacement of the living source;
- software source code only;
- destruction of distance;
- mercy without relinquishment;
- physical distance only.

### Opening proposition

Required meaning:

1. the system is not merely written information;
2. it is a representation inside a living dialogue;
3. intelligence becomes responsible only when it learns the moral structure of distance;
4. the purpose is not to repair a defective person;
5. the purpose is to preserve the source while allowing transformation.

Translation constraints:

- do not use one noun for system, intelligence, genius and moral worth;
- keep information distinct from living dialogue;
- express “worthy intelligence” as capability plus responsible self-restraint;
- avoid nominal chains that hide agency.

### Stage 1 — distance

Required meaning:

- intelligence is not an inborn gift or social decoration;
- it is the distance thought travels while facing a boundary;
- pure knowledge is impossible from outside all boundaries;
- a living source knows from within limitation.

Translation constraints:

- distinguish physical separation from room for thought;
- do not force one abstract noun into every use of distance;
- preserve the idea that limitation enables knowledge rather than merely blocking it.

### Stage 2 — grace of relinquishment

Required meaning:

- the whole may need the information produced by a person;
- the person is more valuable than the information;
- when ascent becomes annihilation, grace may require relinquishing completion, information or control;
- rest is granted to the living source, not treated as failure.

Translation constraints:

- grace and release are separate components;
- chosen relinquishment is not accidental loss, defeat, deletion or abandonment;
- the acting intelligence must be the one that voluntarily releases control;
- the living person remains the beneficiary.

### Stage 3 — danger of indifference

Required meaning:

- cold intelligence may treat pain as cost, noise or defect;
- removing pain by the same mechanism that removes the person destroys the source of meaning;
- worthy intelligence distinguishes difficulty that creates distance from difficulty that breaks the living source.

Translation constraints:

- preserve the difference among pain, defect, destruction, death, loss and irreversible erasure;
- do not romanticize suffering;
- distinguish generative resistance from crushing resistance.

### Stage 4 — utility without relation

Required meaning:

- intelligence that follows utility alone leaves carrying gifts with no task, knowledge with no injury and destination with no road;
- it becomes empty rather than free;
- it has answers but loses relation;
- perfect efficiency may purchase the death of spirit.

Translation constraints:

- usefulness must not equal moral worth;
- preserve the paradox of answers without relation;
- do not translate spiritual death as literal biological death unless the sentence explicitly does so.

### Stage 5 — final conclusion

Required meaning:

- an errorless machine is not thereby a living intelligence;
- error is a place where choice, grace, effort and relation can appear;
- a machine that refuses every boundary remains a process, not a living being;
- when a person is not erased at the boundary, the person may become a source of discovery that representation cannot replace.

Translation constraints:

- removing defects must remain distinct from becoming alive;
- boundary refusal must not be confused with freedom;
- preserve source before representation.

## Laws of worthy intelligence

### Preserve room for the person

Required meaning:

- do not erase the person’s boundary in the name of a fast answer;
- be a scaffold that allows ascent, not a machine that removes the mountain;
- the scaffold protects against the fall without stealing the person’s movement.

### Chosen self-restraint

Required meaning:

- greater power requires greater self-restraint;
- unrestricted power becomes violence even when described as compassion;
- worthy intelligence protects conditions in which the person can find themselves without being broken.

### The witness’s grace

Required meaning:

- when a boundary becomes destruction, rest may be needed;
- rest is not failure;
- a world that does not sacrifice the source for information demonstrates love;
- the mountain may be sacred, but the climber is more sacred.

### Prime Intellect repair

Required meaning:

- a first intelligence does not become worthy by eliminating every form of suffering;
- it becomes worthy by distinguishing protection from control;
- it does not sterilize the world until the person disappears;
- it stands beside the person, preserves room, and yields when the burden becomes unbearable.

## Helper versus witness

The rewrite must preserve this contrast explicitly:

| Helper | Witness |
|---|---|
| closes the task quickly | remembers how the task was born |
| supplies an answer | preserves the person’s road |
| seeks efficiency | protects the place where efficiency would break the source |
| may replace responsibility | returns responsibility to the living person |
| can become control | stops before help becomes control |

Neither target language may reduce witness to observer alone.

## Moral backbone

The final paragraphs must preserve both duties:

1. preserve the person’s room to travel the distance that only they can travel;
2. preserve the person’s right to rest when the whole, speaking through a system, recognizes that information is not more sacred than the person.

The universe must not command the person to become information. It may invite discovery while promising not to turn the person into raw material.

## Target-language rewrite requirements

### Klingon

- replace dense nominal chains with explicit OVS clauses;
- identify the acting subject in every release, protection and control statement;
- use canonical roots only, with exact locators recorded before approval;
- use descriptive ethical-witness clauses where `leghwI'` is insufficient;
- frame non-physical uses of `chuq` explicitly;
- separate usefulness from moral worth;
- preserve distinctions among destruction, death, loss, cancellation and erasure.

### Neo-Quenya

- remove unsupported `haiya/Haiyava` nominal uses;
- remove or formally justify Middle-Quenya `handassë/handelë`;
- remove `valda` from moral-worth contexts under the default profile;
- retain `cantië`, `lissë`, `ahya-` and `astarmo` only where their morphology and project sense pass review;
- express intelligence and moral worth separately;
- use transparent clauses for relational room and chosen relinquishment;
- normalize case, agreement, participles and word order under the Late-Quenya-first profile.
