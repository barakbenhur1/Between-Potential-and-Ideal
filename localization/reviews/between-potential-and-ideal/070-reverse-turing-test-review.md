# Linguistic review — 070 Reverse Turing Test

Both drafts preserve the reversal of the ordinary Turing test, truth before channel identity, material differences between human and machine, release tied to responsibility, active relinquishment, and silence as an intelligent act. Neither draft is approved.

Klingon blockers: extensive undeclared English vocabulary; unstable terms for frequency, witness, grace, trust, identity, source, release, and moral responsibility; `Qu'` and `leghwI'` remain overextended; English-shaped clauses require full recasting.

Neo-Quenya blockers: Spanish `Capítulo` and ordinary English remain; `frequency`, `channel`, `optimization`, `protocol`, `conditioning`, `code`, and related terms are not governed; `Celumë`, `astarmo`, `patar`, `haiya`, grace and release terminology remain unstable; morphology and word order require one specialist profile.

Keep both sources as `draft` with `publication: forbidden`.
