# Linguistic review — 600 Universe, Horizons, Observation, and Access

The source pair preserves the required distinctions:

- cosmology is not used as proof of the theory;
- the observable universe is distinct from the whole universe;
- particle horizon, cosmological event horizon, Hubble radius, and observable universe remain separate terms;
- knowledge remains situated through body, time, memory, language, instruments, and distance;
- cosmological knowledge remains a relation among observation, model, instrument, correction, and uncertainty;
- the oldest observable radiation is not presented as a direct view of the first instant;
- form remains both structure of the thing and structure of access;
- every form reveals some possibilities while obscuring others.

Neither draft is approved. Both contain extensive ordinary English and English-shaped syntax. Cosmology, horizon, observation, signal, access, model, uncertainty, representation, and Potential terminology require specialist normalization under one governed profile for each language. Named horizon types and scientific qualifications must remain exact.
