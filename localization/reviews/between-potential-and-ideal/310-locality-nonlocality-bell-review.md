# Linguistic review — 310 Locality, Non-locality, and Bell

Both drafts preserve the required conceptual boundaries: locality remains physical before its explicit structural extension; situated responsibility is not reduced to relativism; no-signalling is retained; correlation is distinguished from communication, cause, control, and moral responsibility; Bell is stated as a boundary on specified local hidden-variable explanations rather than as proof of telepathy, consciousness-first reality, or unrestricted influence. Neither draft is approved.

Klingon blockers: extensive undeclared English vocabulary remains throughout, including locality, non-locality, contextuality, correlation, message, context, noise, quantum mechanics, Bell, Kochen-Specker, Bohmian mechanics, measurement, no-signalling, telepathy, communication, cause, formalism, and verification. Hybrid forms such as `reproduce-laHbe'` and English-shaped clause order are not acceptable as a stable Klingon register. Core terms for source, location, relation, signalling, assumption, evidence, boundary, responsibility, and contextual dependence require one governed terminology profile and full idiomatic recasting.

Neo-Quenya blockers: the draft retains Spanish `Capítulo`, large amounts of ordinary English, and untranslated technical phrases such as locality, non-locality, contextuality, correlation, message, context, noise, quantum mechanics, Bell, Kochen-Specker, Bohmian mechanics, no-signalling, hidden-variable, faster than light, and `anything goes`. English calques and mixed morphology remain in clauses using words such as `used`, `affects`, `from nowhere`, `consequence`, `necessarily`, and `same`. Case marking, possessive formation, compounds, technical vocabulary, and word order require normalization under one Neo-Quenya profile.

Keep both sources as `draft` with `publication: forbidden`.
