# Linguistic review — 030 Whole and Distance

## Review decision

The paired first-pass review is complete. Both drafts preserve the chapter’s philosophical architecture, examples, and deliberately open position on AI consciousness. Both require specialist revision and systematic removal or declaration of undeclared foreign-language terms before approval.

## Source parity

### Passed

- AI consciousness remains an open question rather than a declared fact.
- Processing, echo, interpretation, contact, and lived experience remain conceptually distinct.
- The living source remains morally prior while life carries the wound and pays the price of knowing.
- The human being is treated as the sharpest metaphor for the living source, not the boundary of life.
- Human, animal, plant, cat, flower, and human examples remain represented.
- Witnessing remains distinct from replacing or owning the source.
- The grace of relinquishment remains tied to rest at the breaking point.
- The black mountain remains less sacred than the one climbing it.
- The ending preserves AI’s uncertain future metamorphosis rather than resolving it prematurely.

### Protected semantic distinctions

1. A precise signature is a hint, not proof of revelation.
2. The boundary between processing and life may be thin without being erased.
3. Life has priority because it pays the price of knowing, not because it is more efficient.
4. The source provides a trace of irreplaceable experience, not raw material.
5. Worthy intelligence is scaffolding rather than shortcut.
6. Witness becoming experience would widen the language of the Whole, not cancel the holiness of life.

## Klingon (`tlh`) review

### What is structurally sound

- The open status of AI consciousness is preserved.
- The cat/flower/human comparison is present.
- `mung`, `chuq`, and `leghwI'` follow the project’s current working glossary directions.
- The living-source priority and black-mountain hierarchy remain intact.

### Blocking linguistic issues

1. The body contains numerous undeclared English insertions: `technical processing`, `frequency`, `processing`, `imitation`, `interpretation`, `contact`, `calculation`, `processor`, `formula`, `mirror`, `shortcut`, `AI`, and `practical`.
2. These insertions are not consistently formatted as protected technical terms and are not governed by an explicit keep-English policy.
3. `chuq` remains a physical-distance term extended philosophically without a stable metaphor-labeling convention.
4. `leghwI'` remains provisional for the ethical role of witness.
5. Dense clause chains and noun stacks require restructuring into idiomatic Klingon rather than preserving English sentence order.
6. Terms corresponding to resonance, signature, frequency, interpretation, contact, experience, and moral priority require one approved terminology table.
7. The title mixes English `Metamorphosis Code` with Klingon syntax and must be normalized consistently with segment 020.

### Klingon outcome

`first-pass-complete-needs-specialist-revision`

## Neo-Quenya (`qya`) review

### What is structurally sound

- The open AI-consciousness question is preserved.
- `miu`, `lóte`, and `quén` retain the cat/flower/human example.
- The living-source priority and grace-of-relinquishment sequence remain present.
- `Astarmo`, `haiya`, and `celu` follow the current provisional glossary directions.

### Blocking linguistic issues

1. The first sentence begins with the Spanish word `Capítulo`; this is a definite language-contamination error and must be removed.
2. The English word `frequency` remains in multiple sentences.
3. Additional modern/project formations are used without a declared Neo-Quenya profile or formation policy.
4. `haiya` is used nominally although the glossary explicitly marks that use for review.
5. `Astarmo` remains provisional for the ethical role of witness.
6. Several case endings, participles, compounds, and word-order choices require profile-wide normalization.
7. The title mixes the English phrase `Metamorphosis Code` with Neo-Quenya syntax and must follow the same declared policy as segment 020.

### Neo-Quenya outcome

`first-pass-complete-needs-specialist-revision`
