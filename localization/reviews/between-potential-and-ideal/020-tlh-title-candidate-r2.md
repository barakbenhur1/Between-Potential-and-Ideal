# Segment 020 Klingon title candidate — round 2

Status: test candidate only. Production source unchanged. Publication forbidden.

## Candidate

`Prime Intellect choHbogh mIw`

`pung: vang yInbogh ghot 'e' chaw' Prime Intellect`

## Back-translation

**The process that changes Prime Intellect**

**Mercy: Prime Intellect permits the living person to act**

## Parse

Main title:

- `Prime Intellect` — object, protected proper name;
- `choH-bogh` — alter/change + relative-clause suffix;
- `mIw` — process and relative-clause head subject.

Subtitle:

- `pung` — mercy;
- `vang` — act or take action;
- `yIn-bogh ghot` — person who lives;
- `'e'` — preceding sentence as object;
- `chaw'` — allow or permit;
- `Prime Intellect` — subject.

## Source record

- `mIw`: canonical “procedure, process”; TKD Addendum and KGT.
- `choH`: canonical “alter, change”; TKD.
- `-bogh`: relative-clause suffix; TKD p.64.
- `pung`: canonical “mercy”; TKD.
- `vang`: canonical “act, take action”; TKD.
- `yIn`: canonical “live”; TKD.
- `ghot`: canonical “person (humanoid)”; TKD.
- `'e'` sentence-as-object construction: TKD pp.65–66.
- `chaw'`: canonical “allow, permit”; TKD. Power Klingon attests `QaghwIj vIQIj 'e' yIchaw'neS`, “Permit me to explain my mistake, your honor.”

## Rejected routes

- `pung chuq je choHmeH chut`: scope does not transparently identify what changes.
- `HeDaj ghoS ...`: `He` is restricted to a physical course or route and cannot carry the chapter's moral or personal-path meaning.
- a causative `choHmoHbogh` version: unnecessary and potentially changes the argument structure; the direct transitive `choHbogh` route is clearer.
