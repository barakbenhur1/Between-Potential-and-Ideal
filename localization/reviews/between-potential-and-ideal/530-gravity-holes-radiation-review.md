# Linguistic review — 530 Gravity, Horizons, Holes, and Radiation

Both drafts preserve the required scientific boundaries: gravity remains an exemplary physical structure rather than will, morality, or intention; limitation can condition world and freedom without making every limit good; black holes remain extreme geometric contraction rather than evil, punishment, or moral agency; white holes remain explicitly hypothetical and limited images rather than proof of creation or Potential itself; the virtual-particle account of Hawking radiation remains an instructional image rather than a literal photograph of the mechanism; statements about vacuum, particles, radiation, observers, and horizons remain cautious; structural precedence does not imply that unactualized possibilities are more real than actual reality. Neither draft is approved.

Klingon blockers: extensive undeclared English remains in the title, headings, and vocabulary of gravity, constraint, orbit, black hole, white hole, event horizon, geometry, contraction, radiation, virtual particle, vacuum, field, distant observer, appearance, and structural precedence. Hybrid constructions and English-shaped syntax remain pervasive. A governed Klingon profile is required for physical limitation, directional geometry, horizons, hypothetical objects, instructional models, radiation, observer-relative appearance, and scientific caution.

Neo-Quenya blockers: the title, headings, and nearly all gravitational and quantum-field terminology remain in ordinary English. English word order and mixed clauses dominate the discussion of gravity, contraction, black holes, white holes, horizons, virtual particles, vacuum, radiation, observers, and structural precedence. Case marking, compounds, verbal morphology, and the distinctions among physical structure, moral metaphor, hypothetical image, instructional account, mechanism, and appearance require normalization under one Neo-Quenya profile.

Keep both sources as `draft` with `publication: forbidden`.
