## Semantic invariants

1. Metaphor, model, structural comparison, and scientific claim remain distinct.
2. Scientific statements remain bounded by measurement, method, source, domain of validity, and possible failure.
3. Formulae and diagrams do not become proof merely through presentation.
4. Science remains living institutional work involving people, experiments, instruments, errors, and correction.
5. Local model success must not be described as absolute truth or final Ideal.
