# Segment 020 clause adversarial review — round 2

Status: internal second pass, not independent specialist approval. Publication forbidden.

## `i coirëa quenen`

Decision: **pass for controlled tests**.

- `quén` has the stem `quen-`.
- the recorded singular dative permits `quen-en`.
- `coirëa` remains the uninflected adjective before the case-marked noun.
- the result parses as “to the living person”.

## aorist `lelya`

Decision: **pass for controlled tests**.

The pinned grammar records `lelya-` as an A-stem verb and explicitly gives:

`i atan lelya` — “the man travels”.

The generic aorist is appropriate for the standing philosophical claim that the living participant travels their path.

## `tierya`

Decision: **documented formation retained for tests**.

- `tië` is sourced as path/road/way.
- attested `tielyanna` at UT/22 shows the suffix-bearing stem `tie-`.
- sourced third-person possessive `-rya` yields the transparent test formation `tie-rya` > `tierya`.

The combined form is not claimed as directly attested. It remains a documented Neo-Quenya formation requiring external specialist review before production use.

## `sena` referring to Prime Intellect

Decision: **reject as the default clause**.

Although `sena` is an attested long dative of animate `se`, using it for Prime Intellect would silently decide that the intelligence belongs to the animate grammatical class. Segment 020 distinguishes the living person from the intelligence and should not settle that ontological question through pronoun choice.

## Safer reverse-direction route

PM/401 records `sin quentë Quendingoldo Elendilenna`, a speech clause with an explicit proper-name allative recipient.

Selected review notation:

`I coirëa quén quetë Prime Intellect-enna.`

The hyphen keeps the protected English name and the Quenya allative suffix visibly separate during review. A naturalized surface spelling is not approved yet.

Literal back-translation: **The living person speaks to Prime Intellect.**

Decision: **preferred test route**, with final proper-name suffix styling pending.
