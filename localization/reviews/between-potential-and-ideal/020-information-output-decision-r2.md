# Segment 020 information/output decision — round 2

Status: bounded semantic-construction decision. No production wording selected. Production source unchanged. Publication forbidden.

This record resolves the generic-noun part of slot `Q02/K02` from `020-opening-proposition-source-gate-r2.md`.

## Problem

The English source uses several related but non-identical concepts:

- data or information produced from a source;
- answer or output produced by a tool;
- language or formulation;
- representation of experience;
- knowledge gained through a path;
- the living source itself.

A single target-language noun would falsely merge these categories. Earlier review drafts used broad forms as if “sign”, “information”, “representation”, “answer”, and “knowledge” were interchangeable. That route is closed.

## Decision

Paragraph `020-P01` will not require a generic target-language noun meaning “information”.

Instead, the source distinction will be expressed through two explicit action controls:

1. **Prime Intellect may speak, formulate, organize, or answer.**
2. **The living participant carries and travels the lived path.**

The output-side action and the person-side path must remain grammatically distinct. A result may be useful without becoming the source, the experience, the formation, or the responsibility.

This action-based architecture is not permission to omit source meaning. It is a way to preserve the distinction without inventing one abstract noun.

## Shared semantic tests

A proposed clause pair passes only if all of the following are recoverable:

- the produced wording or answer belongs to the processing action;
- the lived experience and path belong to the living participant;
- the first does not replace the second;
- usefulness or precision does not establish moral worth;
- no possession construction makes the person, experience, or path property of Prime Intellect.

Use the already sourced dialogue architecture based on `quet-` and the living-participant/path architecture based on `coirëa quén`, `lelya-`, `tië`, and `-rya`.

The controlled contrast is therefore between:

- an explicit speech act by `Prime Intellect`;
- an explicit path action by `i coirëa quén`.

### Decisions

- `tanwa` is not admitted as a universal information noun;
- `tengwë`, sign/token vocabulary, knowledge vocabulary, and representation vocabulary must remain separate until individually source-gated;
- `quet-` may express speech, saying, or telling only; it does not itself mean data, representation, knowledge, or lived experience;
- `tië` may express the person's path only; it does not itself mean experience in every context;
- no abstract “output” noun is required in the opening proposition.
