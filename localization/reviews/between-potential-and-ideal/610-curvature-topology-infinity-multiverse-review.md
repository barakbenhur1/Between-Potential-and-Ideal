# Linguistic review — 610 Curvature, Topology, Infinity, and Multiverse

Both drafts preserve the required distinctions: flat, curved, open, and closed remain precise cosmological terms rather than free metaphors; finite, infinite, boundless, and edged remain logically distinct; curvature and topology remain different questions and local precision does not settle global structure; life-domain analogies remain subordinate to the cosmological distinction; infinity remains distinct from value, meaning, goodness, and Ideal; Potential remains more than quantity and Ideal remains clarification of worthy realization; multiverse remains an umbrella over proposals with different evidential status; omniverse remains cultural, fictional, or metaphorical rather than standard cosmology; multiplicity of possible worlds is not treated as proof or moral permission. Neither draft is approved.

Both drafts retain extensive ordinary English, mixed terminology, and English-shaped syntax. Curvature, topology, finitude, infinity, boundlessness, multiverse, omniverse, evidential status, local/global structure, and domain-of-validity terminology require specialist normalization under one governed profile for each language. Proper scientific names and distinctions must remain exact.
