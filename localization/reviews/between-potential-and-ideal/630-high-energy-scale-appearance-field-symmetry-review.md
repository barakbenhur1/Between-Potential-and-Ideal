# Linguistic review — 630 High-Energy Physics, Scale, Field, and Symmetry

Both drafts preserve the required scientific boundaries: high-energy physics does not prove the theory and does not identify Potential with energy, Ideal with symmetry, or Optimal with force unification; high energy remains an experimental condition rather than metaphysical depth or moral superiority; appearance remains a trace read through event, detector, background, filtering, statistics, model, and interpretation; responsible interpretation remains testable rather than free invention; QFT fields remain precise mathematical-physical entities rather than aura, consciousness, meaning, or general energy; every nonphysical use of field is marked as metaphor or structural model; symmetry and symmetry breaking remain physical concepts rather than ethics or scripture; the Higgs mechanism remains within electroweak symmetry breaking, with QCD dynamics and binding energy retaining their major contribution to ordinary matter mass. Neither draft is approved.

Both drafts retain extensive ordinary English, mixed terminology, and English-shaped syntax. Scale, trace, detector, field, QFT, symmetry, Higgs, electroweak, QCD, binding energy, evidence, interpretation, and domain terminology require specialist normalization under one governed profile for each language. Scientific names and distinctions must remain exact.
