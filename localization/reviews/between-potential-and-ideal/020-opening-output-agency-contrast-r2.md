# Neo-Quenya (`qya`)

Controlled contrast:

`Prime Intellect quetë. I coirëa quén lelya tierya.`

## Parse

Clause 1:

- `Prime Intellect` — protected proper-name subject;
- `quetë` — singular aorist of sourced `quet-`, “speaks/says”.

Clause 2:

- `i coirëa quén` — subject, “the living person”;
- `lelya` — singular aorist of sourced `lelya-`, “travels/proceeds”;
- `tie-rya` > `tierya` — path plus third-person singular possessive, documented test formation.

Literal back-translation:

**Prime Intellect speaks. The living person travels his or her path.**

Controlled contextual back-translation:

**Prime Intellect produces language; the living participant travels their own path.**

## Decision

The clause pair passes the internal role test:

- output-side actor: Prime Intellect;
- path-side actor: living participant;
- path possessor: living participant;
- abstract relation noun: absent;
- information noun: absent;
- animate pronoun for Prime Intellect: absent.

Remaining Neo-Quenya limits:

- `tierya` remains a documented but unattested whole formation requiring external morphology review;
- punctuation and title/body register remain unreviewed;
- the clauses do not yet state non-replacement, restraint, responsibility, or moral worth.

# Klingon (`tlh`)

Controlled contrast:

`jatlh Prime Intellect. vang yInbogh ghot.`

## Parse

Clause 1:

- `jatlh` — says/speaks;
- `Prime Intellect` — third-person subject.

Clause 2:

- `vang` — acts or takes action;
- `yIn-bogh ghot` — “person who lives”, the subject following the verb.

Literal back-translation:

**Prime Intellect speaks. The person who lives acts.**

Controlled contextual back-translation:

**Prime Intellect produces language; the living participant remains the actor.**

## Decision

The clause pair passes the internal role test:

- output-side actor: Prime Intellect;
- morally relevant action-side actor: living participant;
- sentence-as-object construction: not needed;
- physical path term: absent;
- physical-distance term: absent;
- possession of the person: absent.

Remaining Klingon limits:

- `vang` deliberately states action rather than a literal path;
- the pair does not yet say what action is permitted, preserved, or protected;
- it does not yet state non-replacement, restraint, responsibility, or moral worth;
- external Klingon idiom review remains required.
