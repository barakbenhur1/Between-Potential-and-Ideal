# Linguistic review — 400 Architecture, Use, Movement, Light, and Resonance

Both drafts preserve the required boundaries: architecture remains lived form rather than engineering plus decoration; rooms, corridors, windows, and doors remain spatial decisions with human consequences; form and function retain a two-way relation; movement and light remain experiential architectural structures rather than mystical claims; resonance is presented technically first, and every human, social, or theoretical extension is marked as structural analogy rather than physical equivalence. Neither draft is approved.

Klingon blockers: extensive undeclared English remains in the title, headings, formulas, and vocabulary of architecture, use, context, function, movement, space, light, frequency, resonance, structure, rhythm, and analogy. The mixed phrase `elkaar tránh` introduces additional non-Klingon contamination. Hybrid constructions and English-shaped clause order remain pervasive. One governed Klingon profile is required for spatial elements, lived use, form/function relations, movement, light, frequency, resonance, and bounded analogy.

Neo-Quenya blockers: the title, headings, equations, and most architectural terminology remain in ordinary English. English word order and mixed constructions dominate descriptions of rooms, corridors, windows, doors, movement, light, function, frequency, and resonance. Case marking, compounds, verbal morphology, spatial terminology, and the distinction between physical resonance and structural analogy require normalization under one Neo-Quenya profile.

Keep both sources as `draft` with `publication: forbidden`.
