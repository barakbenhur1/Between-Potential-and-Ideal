# Glossary — Klingon stage 2

## Witness — person, act, and literary mode must remain distinct

### Definition

**English source:** A witness does not own the truth. It stands near it, records it, tests it, and remains answerable to what exceeds it. In this project, AI may be a mirror, witness, or tool, but not the owner of lived experience.

**Hebrew cross-check:** עד אינו בעלים של האמת. הוא עומד לידה, מתעד אותה, בודק אותה ונשאר אחראי למה שחורג ממנו. בפרויקט הזה בינה מלאכותית יכולה להיות מראה, עד או כלי, אך לא בעלת החוויה החיה.

**Klingon draft:** `vIt ghajbe' leghwI'. vIt retlhDaq Qam, qon, nuD, 'ej ghaH juvlaHbe'bogh DochvaD jangnIS. jInmolvamDaq 'op retlh 'angbogh jan, leghwI', jan lo'laH je mojlaH AI; 'ach yInbogh SIQghach ghajwI' mojlaHbe'.`

Review risks:

- `leghwI'` means one who sees and may not fully express ethical witnessing.
- `qon` can mean record/compose but must be tested in this context.
- “answerable to what exceeds it” is rendered descriptively and requires revision.

### Project distinctions

| Concept | Working Klingon direction | Status |
|---|---|---|
| witness as person | `leghwI'` | provisional |
| witnessing as act | `leghghach` or a full verbal clause | suffix review required |
| testimony | a phrase built from reporting what was seen/experienced | pending |
| literary witness section | `leghwI' ghItlhmey` | provisional |

The final edition must not use one word indiscriminately for all four meanings.

## Distance and friction

### Definition

**English source:** Distance is the space between answer and understanding. Friction is the resistance through which knowledge becomes lived rather than merely obtained. A shortcut may produce a result while still weakening understanding.

**Hebrew cross-check:** מרחק הוא המרחב שבין תשובה להבנה. חיכוך הוא ההתנגדות שדרכה ידע נעשה חי במקום רק להיאסף. קיצור דרך עשוי לייצר תוצאה ובכל זאת להחליש הבנה.

**Klingon draft:** `jangghach yajghach je joj 'oH chuq'e'. wa' Doch botbogh HoS veghDI' Sov, yInbogh Sov moj; tlhaplu' neHbe'. He ngajmoHlu'chugh, chaq chen gher'ID, 'ach pujchoH yajghach.`

Review notes:

- `chuq` is physical distance; the philosophical extension must be explicitly marked as metaphorical.
- The phrase for friction is descriptive: “a force that resists something.”
- The term for result is provisional and should be reviewed for tone.

### Guardrail

The project does not romanticize suffering. Friction matters only when it preserves learning, contact, responsibility, or lived understanding. Pain by itself is not sacred and does not prove value.

**Klingon draft:** `bechghach quvmoHbe' jInmol. ghojghach, rarghach, Qu', yInbogh yajghach je polDI' neH potlh botghach. lo'laHghach tobbe' 'oy' neH.`

## Metaphor and proof

### Definition

**English source:** Scientific, mathematical, and computational terms are used carefully. When the site makes a formal claim, it must be treated as a claim. When it uses a term as a structural metaphor, the metaphor should clarify rather than pretend to prove.

**Hebrew cross-check:** מונחים מדעיים, מתמטיים וחישוביים משמשים בזהירות. כאשר האתר טוען טענה פורמלית, צריך להתייחס אליה כטענה. כאשר מונח משמש כמטאפורה מבנית, עליו להבהיר ולא להעמיד פנים שהוא מוכיח.

**Klingon draft:** `QeD, mI' QeD, De'wI' QeD je mu'mey lo'lu'taHvIS yepnISlu'. qech SeQ jatlhDI' Daqvam, qech SeQ 'oH net qelnIS. pat ghantoH neH mojDI' mu', qIjmeH lo'nIS ghantoH; tobqanglaw'meH lo'nISbe'.`

### Required labeling rule

Every technical use must be one of:

1. **Formal claim** — accurate, scoped, and sourced.
2. **Structural analogy** — explicitly marked as analogy.
3. **Literary image** — not presented as technical evidence.

**Klingon draft labels:**

- `qech SeQ` — formal claim, provisional.
- `pat rurbogh ghantoH` — structural analogy.
- `lut ghantoH` — literary image.
