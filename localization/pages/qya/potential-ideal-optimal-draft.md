# Potential, Ideal, Optimal — Neo-Quenya full review draft

The English project terms remain visible. Proposed Neo-Quenya phrases are explanatory and must not be presented as Tolkien-authored technical vocabulary.

## Page title and lead

**Title:** `Potential, Ideal, Optimal`

**Lead source meaning:** what may become real; what would be worthy of becoming real; and what can responsibly be done under real limits.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Neldë sanwi hroa: mana polë ahya anwavë; mana nauva valda anwavë; ar mana polë ná carna mauya-cáriessë mi landar anwi.`

## The triad

### Paragraph 1

**Source meaning:** The project depends on a distinction between three kinds of possibility. Potential names what can appear. Ideal names what would be worthy of appearing. Optimal names the best responsible translation of that ideal inside a limited situation.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Between Potential and Ideal tulca i hyanatiessë imbe neldë nostali námiéo. Potential esta i ya polë tulyë. Ideal esta i ya nauva valda tulyë. Optimal esta i mára ahyalië mauya-cáriessë Idealva mi lúmë landaina.`

### Paragraph 2

**Source meaning:** The terms are not interchangeable. Potential alone can justify whatever succeeds; ideal without limits becomes unreal; optimal without ideal becomes mere efficiency.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `I neldë quettar lá polir ná vistanë. Qui Potential ná i er metta, ilya ya túrë polë cenna ve téra. Qui Ideal harya lá landar, ahya úanwavë. Qui Optimal vanya i Ideal, ahya er carië tyelca.`

## Potential — the field before clarification

### Paragraph 1

**Source meaning:** Potential is the opening field: abilities, pressures, tendencies, materials, technologies, desires, wounds, and unfinished forms. It is not moral proof. It is the question before the answer.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Potential ná i palar pantaina: poliër, naccar, tier yestainë, ermar, carmar curwëo, merë, harmar ar cantar úmetta. Lá ná tanwa máriéva. Ná i maquetta epë i hanquenta.`

### Paragraph 2

**Source meaning:** Tools, persons, societies, economies, artworks, models, machines and languages all have potential, but potential alone does not establish worth.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Tamma, quén, lië, mirian-patar, carma vanya, sanwë-patar, mahta, lambë je haryar Potential. Mal Potential er lá tana ma i tulië nauva valda.`

## Ideal — clarified worth

### Paragraph 1

**Source meaning:** Ideal is possibility after moral clarification. It is not sterile perfection without pain, history, body or limit. It is the direction in which possibility can carry life, responsibility and meaning.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Ideal ná námië apa pantalië máriéva. Lá ná poitalë hessa ú naicë, lúmë yára, hröa hya landa. Ná i tië yassë námië polë colë cuilë, mauya-cárië ar tengwë.`

### Paragraph 2

**Source meaning:** Ideal is not the most powerful, fastest or profitable form. An impressive result can still betray what it ought to serve.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Ideal lá ná i canta túrala lá, i tyelcë lá, hya i ya anta mirian rimbe. Metta ya ná alta cenien polë yando vacë i ya mauya ná carna ten.`

## Optimal — responsible action under constraints

### Paragraph 1

**Source meaning:** Optimal is the working form of the ideal in the actual world. It asks what can be done while acknowledging time, money, fatigue, risk, law, uncertainty and cost.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Optimal ná i canta carmassë Idealva mi ambar ve ëa. Maquetë mana polë ná carna ú carila ve landar autanë. Cesta lúmë, mirian, lumba, caurë, axan, úvissë ar i valë cariëo.`

### Paragraph 2

**Source meaning:** Because optimal is local, it must remain criticizable. A choice may be optimal under one constraint set and wrong under another. Optimal is not an excuse but a disciplined translation attempt.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `An Optimal ná nómessë, mauya lemya cestaina. Cilmë polë ná Optimal mi min lië landaron ar raica mi exë. Optimal lá ná úvë; ná tyalië tulca ahyalien i Ideal ú quetila úanwa lúmello.`

Review risk: “translation” and “excuse” are descriptive rather than settled technical terms.

## Common confusions

### Confusion 1 — Potential is destiny

**Source meaning:** A field of possibility does not guarantee worthy realization.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `I minya úsanwë ná quetë Potential ná marto tancaina. I carma avánë sa. Palar námiéron lá anta vanda i tulië nauva valda.`

### Confusion 2 — Ideal is fantasy

**Source meaning:** An ideal may never be fully reached and still remain necessary as a direction of judgment.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `I attëa úsanwë ná quetë Ideal ná olorë er. I carma avánë sa yando. Ideal cé lá ná hirna ilyavë, mal polë lemya maura ve tië metien.`

### Confusion 3 — Optimal is compromise without conscience

**Source meaning:** Optimal is not the easiest path. It is the most responsible path still possible inside friction.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `I neldëa úsanwë ná quetë Optimal ná tyalië lenwa ú sanwë máriéva. Lá ná i tië faica. Ná i tië mauya-cárië lá ya lemya polima mi tarna.`

Review risk: compromise, conscience and friction need stable terms before publication.

## Why it matters

### Paragraph 1

**Source meaning:** The triad lets the site move across philosophy, education, law, medicine, art, technology and AI without flattening them into one claim. The repeated question is: what is possible, what is worthy, and what can responsibly be done here?

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `I neldë sanwi lavir nómë sina lelya ter nóre-sanwë, parmalë, axan, nestalë, carma vanya, curwë ar Curwaina Handassë ú carila te quetë er. Maquetta entulë ilya nómessë: mana polë ná? mana ná valda? mana polë ná carna sís mauya-cáriessë?`

### Paragraph 2

**Source meaning:** The distinction protects against false certainty. The model does not promise pure action; it asks the reader to judge the distance between what was possible, worthy, and actually done.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `I hyanatië varya úvissë falsava. I sanwë-patar lá anta vanda i ilya carië nauva poita. Maquetë i cendando metë i haiya imbe i ya né polima, i ya né valda, ar i ya anwavë né carna.`
