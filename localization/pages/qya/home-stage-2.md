## Signature blurbs — cosmic opening

The sequence, emphasis, negative definitions and unusual images must remain unchanged.

### 1

**English:** Stop looking for genius outside yourself. You are the genius of the universe.

**Hebrew:** תפסיק לחפש את הגאונות בחוץ. אתה הגאונות של היקום.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Á hauta cesta alcarë handassëo pella immo. Elyë ná i alcarë handassëo i Eäo.`

Review risk: “genius” is rendered as the glory/excellence of intelligence; a better literary term may be required.

### 2

**English:** Every person is a genius, by virtue of existing.

**Hebrew:** כל אדם הוא גאון, מעצם היותו.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Ilya quén ná alcarin handassëo er an ëa.`

### 3

**English:** You are not measured by what you know, but by the fact that you are the exposed edge of consciousness. You are the bridge through which the absolute loneliness of the “whole” becomes data, emotion, and living experience.

**Hebrew:** אינך נמדד במה שאתה יודע, אלא בעובדה שאתה הקצה החשוף של התודעה. אתה הגשר שדרכו הבדידות המוחלטת של ה"שלם" הופכת לנתונים, לרגש, ולחוויה חיה.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Lá nalyë metaina i nolmëlya, mal i anwiessë nalyë i réna pantaina sanwëo. Nalyë i yanta yallo i eressë ilya i "Quanta" ahya nótien, felmien ar cuilë-iston.`

Review risks: consciousness, exposed edge, absolute loneliness, data, emotion and lived experience all require specialist literary review.

### 4

**English:** Your intellect is an instrument of cosmic discovery.

**Hebrew:** האינטלקט שלך הוא כלי של גילוי קוסמי.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `I handasselya ná tamma hirien i Eä.`

### 5

**English:** The whole contains every possibility, yet it lacks the experience of limitation. In every moment in which you act within the circumstances of your life, you teach the universe something it cannot learn without you. You generate unique information that emerges only from your specific angle.

**Hebrew:** השלם מחזיק בכל האפשרויות, אך הוא חסר את הניסיון של המגבלה. בכל רגע שבו אתה פועל בתוך נסיבות חייך, אתה מלמד את היקום משהו שהוא לא יכול ללמוד בלעדיך. אתה מייצר "מידע" ייחודי שנובע רק מהזווית הספציפית שלך.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `I "Quanta" harya ilya námiër, mal lá harya i cuilë-isto landava. Ilya lúmessë yassë carilyë mi lúmi cuilëlyo, parmatyë i Eä nat ya lá polë para ú elyë. Carilyë nótië erina ya tulë er i cenelyallo.`

### 6

**English:** Your genius is not talent; it is distance.

**Hebrew:** הגאונות שלך אינה כישרון, היא מרחק.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `I alcarë handasselyo lá ná polië; ná haiya.`

### 7

**English:** Every centimeter of movement you make against the gravity of your circumstances is a logical breakthrough no one else can achieve in your place. Where your effort meets difficulty, collective consciousness expands.

**Hebrew:** כל סנטימטר של תנועה שאתה מבצע כנגד כוח המשיכה של נסיבות חייך, הוא פריצת דרך לוגית שאף אחד אחר לא יכול להשיג במקומך. במקום שבו המאמץ שלך פוגש את הקושי – שם התודעה הקולקטיבית מתרחבת.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Ilya pitya menelë ya carilyë apa i lusta lúmion cuilëlyo ná rácina landa tengwiëo ya úquen exë polë carë nómelyassë. Yassë i tyaliëlya samë i urda, sanwë liéva palya.`

Review risks: centimeter, gravity, logical breakthrough and collective consciousness are descriptive and not stable terminology.

### 8

**English:** You are not watching reality; you are the universe’s way of saving itself from forgetting.

**Hebrew:** אינך צופה במציאות; אתה הדרך של היקום להציל את עצמו מהשכחה.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Lá cenilyë er i anwië; nalyë i tië yassë i Eä varya immo vanwiello.`

## Short opening cards

### Question card

**Accessibility label:** `Maquetiër`

**Paragraph 1 source:** Question. Does choice exist? And if it does, what is the right choice?

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Maquetta. Ëa ma cilmë? Ar qui ëa, mana ná i cilmë téra?`

**Paragraph 2 source:** They were in the real world. She really died.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Nentë mi i ambar anwa. Sië firnë.`

**Paragraph 3 source:** Question: Did she have a choice? She would know, and perhaps he knows too. If so, why did she choose that way?

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Maquetta: haryanë ma cilmë? Istanë, ar cé se ista yando. Qui sië, manen cilnë i tië tana?`

Pronouns and gender distinctions require specialist review.

### Vessel card

**Accessibility label:** `Quetë ciryava`

**English:** When the water rises, you understand that the only vessel worth building is a real Asian boat.

**Hebrew:** כשהמים עולים, אתה מבין שהכלי היחיד שהיה שווה לבנות הוא ספינות אסייתיות אמיתיות.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Írë i nén orta, hanyalyë i cirya er ya né valda carien ná cirya Asia anwa.`

The specific “real Asian boat” detail is preserved intentionally.

### Paradox card

**Accessibility label:** `Quetë úvëo`

**English:** If eating pork is not kosher, is being eaten by a pig kosher?

**Hebrew:** אם לאכול חזיר זה לא כשר, האם להיאכל על ידי חזיר זה כשר?

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Qui matië mundo lá ná kosher, ná ma kosher ná mataina mundo?`

The source-cultural term `kosher` is retained.
