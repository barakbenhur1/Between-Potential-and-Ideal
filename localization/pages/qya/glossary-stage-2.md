# Glossary — Neo-Quenya stage 2

## Witness — person, act, and literary mode must remain distinct

### Definition

**English source:** A witness does not own the truth. It stands near it, records it, tests it, and remains answerable to what exceeds it. In this project, AI may be a mirror, witness, or tool, but not the owner of lived experience.

**Hebrew cross-check:** עד אינו בעלים של האמת. הוא עומד לידה, מתעד אותה, בודק אותה ונשאר אחראי למה שחורג ממנו. בפרויקט הזה בינה מלאכותית יכולה להיות מראה, עד או כלי, אך לא בעלת החוויה החיה.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Astarmo lá harya i anwië. Tarë ara sa, tecë sa, cesta sa, ar lemya hanquenta i yar lahtar sa. Mi carma sina Curwaina Handassë polë ná cilintë, astarmo hya tamma, mal lá haryando cuilë-isto.`

Review risks:

- `Astarmo` names a witness/bystander but may not carry the ethical role required here.
- `cuilë-isto` is a project compound for lived experience.
- the phrase for answerability to what exceeds the witness requires grammatical review.

### Project distinctions

| Concept | Working Neo-Quenya direction | Status |
|---|---|---|
| witness as person | `Astarmo` | provisional |
| witnessing as act | a verbal construction from seeing/attending | pending |
| testimony | a reporting/recording noun distinct from the person | pending |
| literary witness section | `Astarmë` or a descriptive section label | formation under review |

The final edition must not use one form indiscriminately for all meanings.

## Distance and friction

### Definition

**English source:** Distance is the space between answer and understanding. Friction is the resistance through which knowledge becomes lived rather than merely obtained. A shortcut may produce a result while still weakening understanding.

**Hebrew cross-check:** מרחק הוא המרחב שבין תשובה להבנה. חיכוך הוא ההתנגדות שדרכה ידע נעשה חי במקום רק להיאסף. קיצור דרך עשוי לייצר תוצאה ובכל זאת להחליש הבנה.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Haiya ná i imbe hanquenta ar handë. Tarna ná i túrë ya ter nolmë ahya cuilala, lá er mapaina. Tëa sinta polë anta metta ar yando pusta handë.`

Review notes:

- `haiya` is a distance/adverbial direction and its nominal use must be checked.
- `tarna` is used provisionally for resistance/obstacle.
- “knowledge becomes lived” is expressed descriptively, not as a settled idiom.

### Guardrail

The project does not romanticize suffering. Friction matters only when it preserves learning, contact, responsibility, or lived understanding. Pain by itself is not sacred and does not prove value.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `I carma lá airita naicelë. Tarna ná valda er írë varya parma, ovantë, mauya-cárië hya handë cuilala. Naicë er lá ná airë ar lá tana valië.`

Several forms are provisional; the ethical distinction is the priority.

## Metaphor and proof

### Definition

**English source:** Scientific, mathematical, and computational terms are used carefully. When the site makes a formal claim, it must be treated as a claim. When it uses a term as a structural metaphor, the metaphor should clarify rather than pretend to prove.

**Hebrew cross-check:** מונחים מדעיים, מתמטיים וחישוביים משמשים בזהירות. כאשר האתר טוען טענה פורמלית, צריך להתייחס אליה כטענה. כאשר מונח משמש כמטאפורה מבנית, עליו להבהיר ולא להעמיד פנים שהוא מוכיח.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Quettar nolmava, nótessëo ar carma-nótessëo nar yantainë caumavë. Írë nómë sina anta quetë tancaina, mauya ná cendaina ve quetë. Írë quetta ná yantaina ve ve-emma carmava, i emma mauya panta; lá mauya carë ve tana.`

Review risk: technical labels and the construction for structural metaphor need normalization.

### Required labeling rule

Every technical use must be one of:

1. **Formal claim** — accurate, scoped, and sourced.
2. **Structural analogy** — explicitly marked as analogy.
3. **Literary image** — not presented as technical evidence.

**Neo-Quenya draft labels:**

- `Quetë Tancaina` — formal claim, provisional.
- `Ve-emma Carmava` — structural analogy, project phrase.
- `Emma Nyárëo` — literary image.
