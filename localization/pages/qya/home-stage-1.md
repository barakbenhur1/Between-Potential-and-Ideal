## Hero

### Brand

**English:** Between Potential and Ideal  
**Hebrew:** בין פוטנציאל לאידיאל  
**Neo-Quenya:** `Between Potential and Ideal`

The project name remains in English.

### Main heading

**English:** A thought experiment on the moment when possibility begins to take form.  
**Hebrew:** ניסוי מחשבתי על הרגע שבו אפשרות מתחילה לקבל צורה.  
**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Sanwe-tyalië i lúmessë yassë námië yesta harya canta.`

Literal intent: “A thought-play at the time in which possibility begins to have shape.” `námië` and the verbal construction require specialist review.

### Lead

**English:** Here existence is not read as a closed answer, but as movement: from the possible, through witness, choice, and responsibility, toward a more worthy form of life.  
**Hebrew:** כאן הקיום אינו נקרא כתשובה סגורה, אלא כתנועה: מן האפשרי, דרך עדות, בחירה ואחריות, אל צורה ראויה יותר של חיים.  
**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Sís ëa lá ná tancaina hanquenta, mal menelë: námiello, ter astarmo, cilmë ar mauya-cárië, ana canta cuilëo ya ná valda lá.`

Review risks: `mauya-cárië` is a transparent project compound for responsible/obliged action, not an approved dictionary term. Comparative syntax also requires review.

### Method note

**English:** The site offers a reading path: core, witness, and application. Science, poetics, and stories remain distinct, so the idea can be examined without turning into a closed proof.  
**Hebrew:** האתר מציע מסלול קריאה: ליבה, עדות ויישום. המדע, הפואטיקה והסיפורים נשארים מובחנים, כדי שהרעיון יוכל להיבחן בלי להפוך להוכחה סגורה.  
**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Nómë sina anta tië cendien: Endë, Astarmo ar Antacë. Nolmë, lindalë ar nyári lemya hyana, an i sanwë polë ná cestaina ú ahyala tancaina tanomë.`

## Start here

### Heading

`Á yesta sís`

### Paragraph

**English:** This is a bilingual thought experiment combining a philosophical essay, a logical structure, stories, and applications. The recommended path is to start with the summary, continue to the tightened version, and then open the full document or appendices.  
**Hebrew:** זהו ניסוי מחשבתי דו־לשוני שמחבר מסה פילוסופית, מבנה לוגי, סיפורים ויישומים. הדרך המומלצת היא להתחיל בתקציר, לעבור לגרסה המהודקת, ואז לפתוח את המסמך המלא או הנספחים.  
**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Sin ná sanwe-tyalië atta lambion, ya yonta nóre-sanwë, tengwë tengwa, nyári ar antaceli. I mára tië ná yestë i Sinta Quentanen, lelyë i quenta poitaina, ar tá pantë i quenta ilya hya i apendixeli.`

Review risks: the terms for philosophical essay, tightened version and appendices are provisional; `apendixeli` is an explicit temporary loan and must be replaced.

## Required review before stage 2

1. Verify every case ending, agreement form and infinitive construction.
2. Replace temporary compounds and the explicit loan `apendixeli`.
3. Decide whether `Astarmo` can carry the project’s witness/testimony distinction.
4. Approve or reject `námië`, `Curwaina Handassë`, `Antacë`, and `Cómi` for project use.
5. Compare the language against one declared Neo-Quenya grammar profile rather than mixing forms unintentionally.
6. Test every label at the mobile tab-bar width before integration.
