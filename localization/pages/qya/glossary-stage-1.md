# Glossary — Neo-Quenya stage 1

The project terms `Potential`, `Ideal`, and `Optimal` remain visible during review. Proposed Neo-Quenya descriptions are explanatory phrases, not claims that Tolkien supplied exact philosophical equivalents.

## Page title and introduction

### Title

`Quettaparma Sanwion`

Intent: “word-book of concepts/thoughts.” `Quettaparma` and the genitive construction require review.

### Lead

**English source:** A compact guide to the recurring terms of *Between Potential and Ideal*. The definitions are not slogans; they are reading tools for moving through the essays, stories, and applications.

**Hebrew cross-check:** מדריך קצר למושגים החוזרים של “בין פוטנציאל לאידיאל”. ההגדרות אינן סיסמאות, אלא כלי קריאה שמאפשרים לנוע בין המסות, הסיפורים והיישומים.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Sinta téra ná sina i quettaron yar entulir mi Between Potential and Ideal. I pantali lá nar yalmë-quettar; nar tammi cendien ar lelyien ter quentar, nyári ar antaceli.`

Review risk: “slogan” is rendered descriptively as words for public calling.

## Potential — descriptive project term

### Definition

**English source:** Potential is the field of what may become present before it has taken a clarified form. It includes ability, pressure, possibility, unfinished direction, and the surplus from which many different outcomes may appear.

**Hebrew cross-check:** פוטנציאל הוא שדה האפשרויות לפני שהן מקבלות צורה מבוררת. הוא כולל יכולת, לחץ, כיוון לא גמור, עודף אפשרות, ואת מה שממנו תוצאות שונות יכולות להופיע.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Potential ná i palar yar polir tulyë epë haryar canta pantaina. Harya polië, nacca, námië, tië úmetta, ar i úvë yallo mettar rimbi polir tulyë.`

`námië` and `palar` are working directions only. The project concept includes more than personal ability or physical potential.

### Ethical limit

**English source:** Potential is not automatically good. A possibility can become generous, destructive, shallow, beautiful, useful, or false. The ethical question begins when potential receives shape.

**Hebrew cross-check:** פוטנציאל אינו טוב מעצם קיומו. אפשרות יכולה להפוך לדבר נדיב, הרסני, רדוד, יפה, שימושי או שקרי. השאלה המוסרית מתחילה כשהאפשרות מקבלת צורה.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Potential lá ná mára er an ëa. Námië polë ahya antala, rácina, faica, vanya, maita hya falsa. I maquetta máriéva yesta írë Potential harya canta.`

Review risk: several adjectives are provisional or too broad for the exact ethical distinctions.

## Ideal — morally clarified possibility

### Definition

**English source:** Ideal means morally clarified possibility: not simply what can happen, but what would be worthy of becoming real. It is a direction of value, not a decorative fantasy of perfection.

**Hebrew cross-check:** אידיאל הוא אפשרות שעברה בירור של ערך: לא רק מה יכול לקרות, אלא מה ראוי שייעשה ממשי. הוא כיוון של ערך, לא פנטזיה קישוטית של שלמות.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Ideal ná námië apa pantalië máriéva: lá er i ya polë martya, mal i ya nauva valda anwavë. Ná tië valdo, lá olorë vanya ilquava poitavë.`

The English term remains because `valda` alone does not carry the full distinction between worth, direction, and clarified value.

### Guardrail

**English source:** The ideal protects the project from treating every successful realization as justified merely because it succeeded. It asks what a thing ought to become, not only what it can become.

**Hebrew cross-check:** האידיאל מונע מהפרויקט לראות כל מימוש מוצלח כמוצדק רק מפני שהצליח. הוא שואל מה ראוי שדבר יהפוך להיות, לא רק מה הוא יכול להיות.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Ideal varya i carma lá quetila ilya tulië ya túra ná téra er an túrë. Maquetë mana nat mauya ahya, lá er mana polë ahya.`

## Optimal — local translation under constraints

### Definition

**English source:** Optimal is the local translation of the ideal under real constraints. It is what can responsibly be done inside time, body, budget, law, fear, uncertainty, fatigue, and incomplete knowledge.

**Hebrew cross-check:** אופטימלי הוא התרגום המקומי של האידיאל תחת מגבלות ממשיות. זה מה שאפשר לעשות באחריות בתוך זמן, גוף, תקציב, חוק, פחד, חוסר ודאות, עייפות וידע חלקי.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Optimal ná i ahyalië nómessë Idealva mi landar anwi. Ná i ya polë ná carna mauya-cáriessë mi lúmë, hröa, mirian landaina, axan, caurë, úvissë, lumba ar nolmë lá quanta.`

Review risks: “local translation,” budget and responsibility are project constructions and require normalization.

### Guardrail

**English source:** The optimal is not a surrender of the ideal. It is the attempt to carry the ideal without pretending that the world has removed all friction.

**Hebrew cross-check:** האופטימלי אינו ויתור על האידיאל. הוא הניסיון לשאת את האידיאל בלי להעמיד פנים שהעולם הסיר את כל החיכוך.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Optimal lá ná antanië i Ideal. Ná i tyalië colien i Ideal ú carila ve i ambar autanë ilya nacca ar tarna.`

## Source — `celu`, with the living origin kept visible

### Definition

**English source:** The source is the living origin before extraction: the person, experience, scene, wound, question, or reality from which a statement is taken. The project repeatedly warns against honoring an idea while forgetting the living source that made it possible.

**Hebrew cross-check:** המקור הוא הראשית החיה לפני החילוץ: האדם, החוויה, הסצנה, הפצע, השאלה או המציאות שמהם נלקחה אמירה. הפרויקט מזהיר שוב ושוב מפני כיבוד הרעיון תוך שכחת המקור החי שאפשר אותו.

**Neo-Quenya draft:** `Celu ná i yesta cuina epë mapalië: i quén, i cuilë-isto, i cene, i harma, i maquetta hya i anwië yallo quetë ná mapaina. I carma enyalë atalantavë i cauma quetien sanwë ar vanwila i celu cuina ya carnë sa polima.`

### Project distinction

- `celu` is the working term for source/origin.
- `cuina celu` is the working phrase for living source.
- The source is not merely a citation entry or extracted data object.
- The glossary must preserve the priority of the living origin over possession of the extracted idea.

2. Approve or replace `námië`, `mauya-cárië`, `palar`, and other project formations.
3. Decide whether the English project terms remain visible beside Neo-Quenya descriptions.
4. Test `celu` and `cuina celu` in philosophical, technical, and literary contexts.
5. Only then continue to Witness, Distance/Friction, and Metaphor/Proof.
