## Source policy

- KLI lists `'a'baqa'` as “file, dossier, portfolio,” from qep'a' 31.
- KLI accepts only confirmed Marc Okrand sources in its additions list; the published dictionaries remain necessary.
- Eldamo explicitly labels the working mixed-period vocabulary Neo-Quenya and marks fan-created terms with `!`.
- Relevant Eldamo entries include `cómë`, `astarmo`, `celu`, `lé`, `quenta`, `nyárë`, and `antac-`.

A label is approved only after vocabulary, grammar, full navigation context, mobile layout, breadcrumbs and accessibility labels are reviewed.
