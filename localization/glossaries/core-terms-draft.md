# Core terminology - working draft

Status: linguistic research. None of the target-language terms in this file is approved for broad publication yet.

The English term defines the project concept. Hebrew is used as a semantic cross-check, not as an intermediate translation. Where no safe one-word equivalent exists, the preferred direction is a transparent descriptive phrase.

## Approval rule

A term becomes approved only after it has been tested in at least three contexts:

1. a short interface label;
2. a philosophical explanatory paragraph;
3. a literary or metaphorical sentence.

The approved form, rejected alternatives and source references will then be copied into the production glossary.

## Research references

- Klingon vocabulary and grammar: Marc Okrand's canonical publications, with later confirmed vocabulary tracked by the Klingon Language Institute.
- Neo-Quenya vocabulary and grammar: Tolkien's published linguistic material, cross-checked through Eldamo and its explicit Neo-Quenya labeling.
