# Localization glossaries

Klingon and Neo-Quenya translations must use reviewed terminology consistently.

Each glossary entry must record:

- the English source term;
- the Hebrew semantic cross-check;
- the intended meaning in this project;
- the approved Klingon rendering;
- the approved Neo-Quenya rendering;
- rejected alternatives or uncertainty notes when relevant.

Do not invent an undocumented target-language word merely to preserve a one-word source term. Prefer attested vocabulary or a transparent descriptive phrase. The brand name `Between Potential and Ideal` remains in English.

Initial terms to resolve before broad translation:

- Potential
- Ideal
- Optimal
- Witness
- Living source
- Distance and friction
- Artificial intelligence
- Reverse Turing test
- Grace of relinquishment
