## Overrides

### Worthy / moral worth

- Klingon: `lo'laH` must not be treated as a complete equivalent of moral worth. Use a sourced descriptive clause that separates usefulness, capability and responsible conduct.
- Neo-Quenya: `valda` is not approved for moral worth under the Late-Quenya-first profile because the recorded Late-Quenya sense conflicts with the older worthy direction.

### Distance

- Klingon: `chuq` is restricted to physical distance unless a philosophical extension is made explicit and passes all context tests.
- Neo-Quenya: `haiya/Haiyava` is rejected as an abstract noun for distance. `hairië` is fan-invented and requires a documented exception plus independent approval.

### Witness

- Klingon: `leghwI'` is observer-like only; it does not automatically express the ethical witness role.
- Neo-Quenya: `astarmo` is a sourced candidate for bystander/witness, but the ethical role still requires contextual qualification. `cenindo` is not the active direction.

### Exceptional intelligence / AI

- Neo-Quenya: `handassë` and `handelë` are not accepted automatically because the recorded forms are Middle Quenya. `curwaina handassë` remains mechanical draft wording only.
- Both languages: cognitive capacity, exceptional skill, wisdom and moral self-restraint must be translated separately.

### Grace and relinquishment

- Klingon: `pung` is at most a mercy-direction candidate. The voluntary release component requires its own sourced clause.
- Neo-Quenya: `lissë` is source-confirmed for grace, but it does not by itself encode chosen relinquishment. Research directions such as `apsen-` and `hehta-` remain unapproved.

### Living source

- Klingon: `yInbogh mung` is provisional pending source and syntax review.
- Neo-Quenya: `ehtelë` literally refers to a spring/source. Any construction using it for a living person or origin is metaphorical and must be marked and tested.

### Protection and control

- Protection must preserve agency and a route of return.
- Control replaces or captures agency.
- The two concepts must use distinct constructions in both target languages.

## Propagation prohibition

No form listed in this override may be propagated repository-wide until:

1. source and period are recorded;
2. morphology and syntax pass review;
3. label, philosophical and literary tests pass;
4. English/Hebrew parity passes;
5. an independent second reviewer approves;
6. all repository occurrences are classified;
7. mechanical audit remains zero after propagation.
