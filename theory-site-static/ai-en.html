AI

Dialogues with artificial intelligence as linguistic and logical testing.

Language and reading

This page is in English. The language switch points to the parallel page when one exists; source documents are kept unchanged.

Critical caution

Information is not experience, simulation is not living witness, and linguistic response is not belief.

How to read the AI section

The dialogues here do not claim that a machine has experience or belief. They work as a linguistic and logical mirror: a way to test how ideas return to us after passing through a system that calculates language without living it.
